## What was built (2026-10-15)

- **LSA pass** (Runs and keystones card, under "Edit this run's scene text below"): pick an LSA run, press LSA PASS. Server: POST /api/lsa/weave on 8000, function weave_lsa_into_scene in domain_engine.py. It weaves the LSA analysis's materials, condition and biology into the selected pipeline run's scene text; structure stays the scene text's; light, weather, season, compass words and recalled structure are left out and listed under "What the LSA pass left out" (set_aside, not_added). LSA species replace the ecology record. UNDO restores the text.
- **A/B KEYSTONES** (under REPLAY RUN): two keystone candidates from the selected run, same seed and pano. A = the scene text at the moment the LSA pass ran; B = the text now. Labelled "A/B A" and "A/B B (LSA)", linked to each other. Warns if the finishing pass ran after the LSA pass, or B was hand-edited after it.
- **View stamping of LSA runs**: an LSA run made with the dial Linked and the Cesium camera exactly on a saved view records saved_view_id; the LSA pass dropdown lists those first ("Made at this view") and picks the newest.
