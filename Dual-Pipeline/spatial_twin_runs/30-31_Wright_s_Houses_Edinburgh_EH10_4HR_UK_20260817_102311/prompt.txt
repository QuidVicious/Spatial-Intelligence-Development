An elevated documentary photograph capturing Wright’s Houses in Bruntsfield, Edinburgh, angled west-northwest from above Bruntsfield Links. The foreground features the sloping green turf of the Links and slender bare tree trunks. Across the quiet asphalt street, traditional multi-storey Scottish tenements stand shoulder-to-shoulder, anchored by the distinct painted frontage of The Golf Tavern with its historic stone trim, multi-pane sash windows, and steep slate gables flanked by taller blonde sandstone tenement blocks. Soft, diffused Scottish daylight casts clean, gentle shadows across the aged stone masonry, chimney pots, and slate roofs under a pale overcast sky.
