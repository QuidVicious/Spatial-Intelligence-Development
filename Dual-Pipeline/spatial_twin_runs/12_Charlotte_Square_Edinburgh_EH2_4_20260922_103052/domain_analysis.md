# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Calciferous Sandstone Measures of the Lothian Lower Carboniferous stratigraphy, overlain by dense glacial till. Lithics comprise fine-to-medium-grained Carboniferous Craigleith sandstone (high quartz-arenite ashlar) and Hailes sandstone foundations, bound with traditional slaked lime and coarse river sand mortars. Subterranean moisture is moderated by natural westerly slope drainage towards the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban ensemble featuring Robert Adam's unified palace-front scheme and Robert Reid's West Register House (former St George's Church). West Register House presents a rusticated ground floor, giant-order tetrastyle portico, stepped attic, and a monumental drum supporting an octagonal-domed cupola and lantern. Flanking terraces are three-storey plus rusticated basements and attic mansards, with regular fenestration grids (6-over-6 and 12-over-12 timber sashes), cast-iron decorative balconettes, and slate-clad roof pitches with stone chimney stacks. Foreground features Sir John Steell’s Albert Memorial on a multi-tiered stone plinth. Planar facades rectified to vertical alignment.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Primary masonry: Fine-dressed, smooth-tooled pale buff-to-grey Craigleith sandstone ashlar with channel-rusticated base levels; patinated copper dome exhibiting verdigris green oxidation. Slate roofs with lead flashings. Plinth of the central equestrian monument comprises tooled pink Peterhead granite and buff sandstone supporting a verdigris-patinated cast bronze sculpture. Weathering patterns include localized atmospheric sulphate crusts, soot soiling in protected friezes, and rain-washed clean ashlar planes.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Parkland specimen trees dominated by *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Platanus × acerifolia* (London Plane), and *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved Lime). Late-December winter phenology: complete dormancy, defoliated branch structures, bare twig canopies, and dormant perennial turf grass (*Lolium perenne* / *Festuca rubra*) forming the central garden green.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry-generated artifacts, transient vehicles, parking arrays, pedestrians, and temporary street furniture are removed. Permanent civil fabric (cast-iron perimeter railings, stone kerbs, and monument pedestal) is fully preserved and restored to structural integrity.
