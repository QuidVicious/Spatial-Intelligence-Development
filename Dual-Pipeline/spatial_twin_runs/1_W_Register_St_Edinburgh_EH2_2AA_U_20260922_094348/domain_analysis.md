# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary sequences (Calciferous Sandstone Measures) overlain by glacial till.
- Lithic Fabric: Local buff-blonde Craigleith and Hailes sandstones; accents of Permian red Locharbriggs sandstone; Caithness stone paving flags; Scottish whinstone and granite kerbs/setts.
- Mortar & Subterranean: Non-hydraulic to feebly hydraulic hot-mixed lime mortars with sharp silica sand; stable engineered urban subterranean drainage across the New Town ridge.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Central Civic Focus: Melville Monument (1821–1828); colossal fluted Roman Doric column on a rusticated stepped square pedestal, crowned with a standing stone figure.
- Garden Layout: Elliptical and circular geometric path networks enclosing lawn panels and a sunken pavilion.
- Perimeter Typologies: Neo-Classical Georgian ashlar terraces (3–4 storeys over rusticated basements, timber sash fenestration); Victorian commercial banks with Corinthian/Ionic giant orders; 20th-century Portland stone/granite clad corporate blocks (5–7 storeys, modular recessed grids).
- Linear Geometry: Rectified true vertical facades, crisp parapets, pitched slate roofs with lead flashings, and cast-iron balustrades.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Fine-grained blonde Craigleith sandstone ashlar; rusticated ashlar basements; polished granite plinths; Portland limestone panels.
- Secondary Elements: Dark Scottish slate pitched roofs; cast-iron perimeter boundary railings and lamp standards; bronze and patinated copper roof caps.
- Weathering & Patina: Historical atmospheric soot deposition in protected mouldings; light rain-washed friezes; clean lime mortar repointing; slight biological patina on shaded stone bases.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Urban Canopy: Perimeter and interior groves of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved Lime), and *Betula pendula* (Silver Birch).
- Seasonal Phenology (Late September): Early autumn canopy transition; mature green foliage showing focal crown thinning and early yellowing chlorosis across outer leaf margins; neat perennial borders; dense, manicured civic turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Complete elimination of all transient cars, double-decker buses, delivery vans, pedestrians, temporary scaffolding wraps, traffic cones, and street refuse. Static permanent infrastructure (tram tracks, stone kerbs, iron lampposts, fixed seating) preserved.
