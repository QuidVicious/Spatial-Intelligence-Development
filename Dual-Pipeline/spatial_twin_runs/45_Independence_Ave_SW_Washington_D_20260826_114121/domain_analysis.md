# Spatial Domain Analysis: 45 Independence Ave SW, Washington, DC 20515, USA

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
The site occupies Jenkins Hill (Capitol Hill), situated immediately along the Atlantic Seaboard Fall Line. Subterranean stratigraphy consists of unconsolidated Cretaceous fluvial-marine deposits of the Potomac Formation (dense clays, gravel lenses, and quartz sands) resting unconformably atop late Precambrian to early Paleozoic crystalline metamorphic Piedmont basement rocks. 

The primary above-ground structural lithics comprise:
- **Central Core:** Historic Aquia Creek Cretaceous sandstone from Stafford County, Virginia, coated in preservation whitewash/paint coats to mitigate porous freestone spalling.
- **North & South Senate/House Wings:** Fine-grained crystalline Cockeysville/Texas marble and Lee (Massachusetts) dolomitic marble ashlar blocks with fine-tooled lime-putty mortar joints.
- **Plinths and Subterranean Retaining Foundations:** Load-bearing Wissahickon schist rubble footings and dense granite sub-plinths.
- **Superstructure Dome:** Structural cast iron (aggregate ~8.9 million lbs) bolted and riveted to internal load-bearing masonry piers and brick core vaults.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Typology:** Neoclassical civic monumentalism (Greek Revival and Roman Classicism).
- **Massing & Structural Hierarchy:** 
  - **Center:** Central rotunda drum flanked by the historic sandstone core, surmounted by Thomas U. Walter’s two-tier cast-iron peristyle dome, ribbed tholos, lantern cupola, and Thomas Crawford's bronze *Statue of Freedom* (apex elevation ~88 m / 288 ft).
  - **South Flank (Foreground Right):** The House Wing, a rectangular 3-storey plus rusticated English basement block featuring colossal Corinthian order colonnades, pedimented window aedicules, and horizontal entablatures terminating in dentiled modillion cornices.
  - **North Flank (Distant Left):** Senate Wing, mirroring the symmetrical footprint of the House Wing.
  - **West Front (Left/Center Facing the Mall):** Stepped Olmsted marble terraces with formal dual-flight balustraded staircases descending to the West Mall lawns.
- **Planar Rectification & Geometric Truth:** All colonnade shafts plumbed to true gravitational vertical (90.0°). Elimination of photogrammetry skew across horizontal architraves, balustrade copings, and flat standing-seam copper terrace roofs. Complete unwarping of fenestration grids and rusticated basement arcades.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Dome Superstructure:** Multiple historic protective layers of high-grade zinc/alkyd paint over structural gray-iron castings, displaying faint specular sheen under direct sunlight and subtle rain-run micro-patina in concave flutings.
- **Marble Wings:** Brilliant light-gray to soft white crystalline marble ashlar with faint calcitic veining. Sheltered under-soffits, column capital volutes, and lower water-table ledges exhibit subtle historic carbon wash lines and mild atmospheric graying characteristic of the mid-Atlantic temperate climate.
- **Terraces & Roofs:** Standing-seam oxidised patinated copper sheet roofing (matte viridian green / verdigris hues) on wing roofs; pale sandstone and granite pavers across the West Terraces with precise dry-laid jointing.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
The surrounding Capitol Grounds (Olmsted landscape design) feature mature, multi-layered temperate urban arboriculture:
- **Deciduous Overstory:** Towering *Platanus occidentalis* (American sycamore), *Quercus alba* (White oak), *Quercus phellos* (Willow oak), *Liriodendron tulipifera* (Tulip tree), and *Ginkgo biloba*.
- **Midstory & Buffers:** Dense broadleaf canopies of *Celtis occidentalis* (Hackberry), *Tilia americana* (American basswood), and pruned *Buxus sempervirens* (English boxwood) parterres bordering the paved plazas.
- **Ground Cover:** Manicured *Poa pratensis* (Kentucky bluegrass) turf lawns presenting deep emerald saturation, fully disambiguated from solid stonework.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All temporary construction netting, scaffolds, transient pedestrian crowds, tour groups, passenger vehicles, delivery vans, jersey barriers, security stanchions, and service dumpsters are entirely removed. Retained permanent infrastructure includes:
- Historic cast-iron Washington globe lamp posts and bronze bollards.
- Sandstone curbs, granite perimeter coping walls, and paved red-granite aggregate plazas of the Capitol perimeter.
- Symmetrical paved pedestrian promenades and historic Olmsted fountain basins.
