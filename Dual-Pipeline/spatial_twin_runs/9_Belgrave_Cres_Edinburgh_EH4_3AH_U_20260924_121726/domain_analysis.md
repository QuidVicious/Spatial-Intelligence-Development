# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary strata (sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone) interspersed with igneous dolerite intrusions along the Water of Leith valley. Structural masonry composed of local Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartz-rich sandstone. Bedding laid with non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic slaked lime mortar. Sloping alluvial gravels and glacial till ensure rapid urban runoff.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left: Victorian residential tenement block; planar vertical rectification, three-storey massing with projecting canted multi-tier bay windows, molded stone cornices, and timber 2-over-2 sash-and-case fenestration grids.
Center: Gothic Revival ecclesiastical structure; rectangular nave with buttressed bays, pointed-arch fenestration, and a prominent square bell tower topped with crenellated parapets and corner pinnacles.
Surrounds: Low dressed-sandstone boundary retaining walls along the road corridor.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Residential block: Fine-grained Craigleith blonde sandstone ashlar with fine-tooled droved margins, cast-iron rainwater downpipes, and pitched Scotch slate rooflines.
Ecclesiastical tower/nave: Weathered grey-buff coursed squared sandstone rubble with droved ashlar dressings, lead flashing, and slate roofing.
Pathology: Localized atmospheric coal-smoke crusts, dark runoff staining along stringcourses, and damp biological greening on lower plinths.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Urban woodland margin featuring *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Late-November phenology exhibits full autumn defoliation, revealing bare, intricate branch architectures with sparse residual brown leaf clusters and ground-level leaf litter along kerbs.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh noise, transient parked vehicles, and street-level artifacts are eliminated. Preserves authentic static roadway asphalt, whinstone kerbing, stone retaining walls, and fixed architectural envelopes.
