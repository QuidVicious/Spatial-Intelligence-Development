# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Strathclyde Group (sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone) overlain by dense glacial till. Primary structural masonry is Craigleith sandstone (a highly siliceous, quartzitic buff-grey pale sandstone with high compressive strength) combined with Hailes sandstone for structural backing. Mortar is traditional non-hydraulic slaked lime and coarse river sand. The central equestrian monument plinth is composed of polished pink Peterhead granite blocks set upon a hard sandstone sub-base.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban square composition dominated by the central monument of West Register House (formerly St George's Church). The central edifice presents an imposing tetrastyle Ionic portico with unfluted/fluted giant order columns, an unadorned frieze, triangular pediment, and a monumental central copper dome supported on an arcaded drum with peristyle Corinthian colonnettes and an octagonal lantern cupola. Flanking wings display symmetrical, three-storey plus attic and basement Georgian townhouse facades: rusticated ashlar ground storeys with round-arched openings, piano nobile with moulded architraves and pedimented central bays, regular 6-over-6 and 12-pane timber sash-and-case fenestration, projecting continuous stone stringcourses, cornice entablatures, balustraded parapets, dormer-punctuated Mansard roofs, and tall ashlar chimney stacks. Planar surfaces rectified to true vertical alignment.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House: Finely dressed buff Craigleith ashlar, copper-sheathed dome with extensive blue-green copper carbonate/sulfate verdigris patina, cast lead flashings, and cast bronze statuary/urns.
- Residential Wings: Fine-droved Craigleith sandstone ashlar, rusticated V-joint ashlar basements, Welsh and Scottish slate roofing tiles, wrought iron area railings, and lead-dressed dormers.
- Albert Memorial: Cast dark patinated bronze equestrian figure and relief plaques mounted on rusticated and polished Peterhead pink granite blocks.
- Environmental Weathering: Localized carbonaceous soiling and gypsum crusts beneath cornice undercuts, rainwater wash-lines revealing pristine buff quartz grain on exposed pediment faces, and faint verdigris wash staining on upper stone drums.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Charlotte Square Garden interior dominated by mature broadleaf specimens: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm). Late September phenology: dense canopies exhibiting early autumn senescence, chlorotic pale-yellow to ochre leaf margins, patchy crown thinning, and initial leaf-litter dispersal across the manicured *Lolium perenne* and *Festuca rubra* turf lawn.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh noise, transient street clutter, traffic, vehicles, pedestrians, and temporary barriers removed. Retained elements: cast-iron perimeter spearhead railings, stone kerbs, paved walkways, historic cast-iron gas-style streetlamp standards, and the central Prince Albert bronze memorial on its granite pedestal.
