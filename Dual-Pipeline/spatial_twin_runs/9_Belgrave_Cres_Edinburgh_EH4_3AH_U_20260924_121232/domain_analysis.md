# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock with volcanic basalt intrusions. Superstructure lithics composed of local pale grey and buff Craigleith and Hailes sandstones. High-calcium hydraulic lime mortar joints. Sloped glacial till and alluvial drainage towards the adjacent Water of Leith valley.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Plumbed multi-storey Victorian residential terrace in late classical/Italianate style featuring canted bay windows, molded stone cornices, and rusticated basements. Aligned fenestration of 2-over-2 and 1-over-1 timber sash-and-case windows. Midground Gothic Revival ecclesiastical tower with vertical buttressing, louvred belfry lancets, and decorative corner pinnacles. Rectified stone boundary retaining walls with perimeter cast-iron railings.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Fine-grained Craigleith sandstone ashlar with stugged and polished dressings, displaying authentic industrial coal-smoke patina and rain-wash banding. Scottish and Welsh slate pitched roofing with lead flashings and dressed stone chimney stacks. Rolled asphalt carriageway bounded by whinstone and granite kerbs alongside Caithness stone flagstone pedestrian pavements. Wrought- and cast-iron spearhead railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban woodland canopy of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), *Ulmus glabra* (wych elm), and *Tilia × europaea* (common lime) rooted within the terraced gardens. March 24 phenology: completely bare deciduous canopies with dense, intricate branch structures, swelling dormant vegetative buds, and exposed dark bark fissures.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh noise, distorted roadside vehicles, transient pedestrian figures, and road debris purged. Pavements, carriageway, stone retaining walls, ironwork, and structural facades fully rectified and cleared.
