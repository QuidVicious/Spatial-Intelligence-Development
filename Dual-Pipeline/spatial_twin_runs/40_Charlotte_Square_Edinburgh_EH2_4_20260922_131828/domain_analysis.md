# Spatial Domain Analysis: 40 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Strathclyde Group (sandstone, siltstone, shale) overlaid by dense Devensian glacial till. Primary structural masonry is fine-grained, silica-cemented pale-buff Craigleith sandstone. Monument pedestal utilizes polished red Peterhead granite. Mortar consists of traditional hot-mixed calcitic non-hydraulic lime and sharp sand.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Central monumental neo-classical church (West Register House, formerly St George's Church) features a tetrastyle Ionic portico, dentiled pediment, rusticated podium, two-stage square tower base, circular peristyle drum with Corinthian columns, and a ribbed hemispherical copper dome capped by an open lantern. Flanking neo-classical terraced townhouses (Robert Adam design) present unified 3-storey-plus-attic facades over rusticated ground-floor arcades, channelled ashlar lintel courses, mutule cornices, pitched slate roofs with dressed stone chimneystacks, and uniform 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case fenestration.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House: Ashlar-dressed pale Craigleith sandstone; oxidized copper dome with verdigris patina; cast-lead flashing.
- Flanking Terraces: Droved and polished Craigleith ashlar, rusticated sandstone ground floors; Scottish Ballachulish grey-black roofing slates; cast-iron rainwater goods.
- Albert Memorial: Polished pink-red Peterhead granite ashlar pedestal; statuary cast in patinated dark-brown and oxidized green bronze.
- Enclosure: Wrought and cast-iron perimeter railings with urn and spearhead finials over dressed sandstone plinth kerbs.
- Weathering: Localised atmospheric sulphate crusts in sheltered cornices, biological green algae at stone footings, rain-washed clean ashlar fascias.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban park canopy within garden enclosure: *Ulmus glabra* (wych elm), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore maple), and *Tilia × europaea* (common lime). Late-September phenology: dense crowns transitioning to early chlorosis, featuring mottled yellow-green margins, localized leaf senescence, and minor canopy thinning over mown perennial rye-grass (*Lolium perenne*) and meadow-grass (*Poa pratensis*) lawn turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicular traffic, parked cars, modern street signage, dumpsters, and pedestrians are removed. Fixed historical infrastructure (cast-iron streetlamps, stone kerbs, iron railings) is preserved.
