## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics

- Masonry Lithics: Craigleith and Cullalo blonde-to-buff Carboniferous sandstones across Victorian and neoclassical ashlar facades; modern reconstituted/precast Portland-composite stone panels on contemporary infills; Ballachulish metamorphic slate roof tiling.
- Mortar Chemistry: Traditional hot-mixed hydraulic lime mortar (non-hydraulic calcium hydroxide with volcanic pozzolanic additives) on historic masonry; contemporary polymer-modified Portland cement and elastomeric structural silicone seals on modern curtain walling and zinc/lead flashing interfaces.
- Hydrology & Subterranean Dynamics: Well-drained urban strata sloping southward into the Waverley Valley basin, managed via subterranean masonry culverts and modern cast-iron storm runoffs.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Typology: Dense urban commercial block at Princes Street and South St Andrew Street, representing a stratified collage of 19th-century Victorian/Edwardian French Renaissance Mansard corner architecture, contemporary 21st-century modular stone grid curtain facades, and integrated rooftop modernist glass-box/zinc plant pavilions.

  - Modern Eastern Wing: 6 to 7 storeys of crisp, deeply recessed rectilinear sandstone/precast grid fenestration with a clean horizontal parapet line.
  - Rooftop Extensions: Stepped-back 2-storey modernist rectilinear glass-and-zinc vertical pavilions housing plant and high-spec office space.
  - Surrounding Victorian Tenements / Commercial Palazzos (Background / Forefront): 4 to 6 storeys with chimney stacks, coped parapets, flat asphalt and pitched slate roofs.
