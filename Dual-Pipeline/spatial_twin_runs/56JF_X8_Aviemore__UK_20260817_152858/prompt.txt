High-angle 35mm telephoto documentary photograph of a working rural farmstead nestled in the Speyside countryside near Aviemore, Scotland, viewed looking west-southwest from an elevated vantage point. 

In the foreground, a traditional Scottish 1.5-storey whitewashed steading cottage stands with plumb, crisp lime-harled stone walls, dormered Ballachulish slate roof, and clean gables. Across the rutted, gravelled farmyard stands an expansive agricultural barn complex clad in weathered corrugated profiled sheeting with translucent rooflights, flanked by rusted tin-roofed sheds and farm machinery. Compacted dirt and crushed aggregate tracks cut through vibrant green undulating pastureland.

Early August morning sunrise atmosphere: low-angled warm golden sunlight (3400K) streams from the eastern horizon behind the camera, casting elongated, sharp soft-edged shadows across the dewy turf and yard. Cool, crystal-clear Highland morning air, pristine geometric clarity, crisp architectural edges, natural atmospheric depth, authentic documentary realism, shot on Kodak Portra 400 with a 50mm architectural tilt-shift lens.
