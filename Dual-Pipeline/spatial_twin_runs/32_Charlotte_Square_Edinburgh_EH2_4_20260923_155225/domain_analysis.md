# Spatial Domain Analysis: 32 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Calders Member sedimentary siltstone and sandstone overlain by glacial till. Masonry lithics: Pale buff-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstone (carboniferous quartz-arenite). Mortar: Traditional hot-mixed hydraulic lime with graded quartz sand. Natural sub-surface drainage toward the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical public monument and Georgian residential square. Centerpiece: West Register House (former St George's Church, Robert Reid, 1814) featuring a monumental tetrastyle Ionic portico, rusticated podium, square clock tower, and a ribbed circular copper dome on a stepped drum. Flanking: Four-storey Georgian terraced townhouses (Robert Adam design) featuring rusticated ground floors, smooth ashlar upper levels, continuous stringcourses, modillioned cornices, pitched slate roofs with dormers, and regular 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith fine-grained ashlar sandstone with selective coal-smoke darkening and water run-off weathering.
- Dome: Standing-seam copper sheet with mature verdigris green oxidation patina.
- Roofs: Ballachulish blue-grey roofing slate in diminishing courses with lead ridge flashing and dressed stone chimney stacks.
- Ground: Bituminous road surfacing, fine-grained silver-grey granite kerbs, flagstone pedestrian footpaths, and mown perennial rye grass lawns. Cast-iron spearhead railings atop sandstone dwarf boundary walls.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Species: *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) within Charlotte Square Gardens. Phenology (late December): Complete winter defoliation, fully exposed ramified branch scaffolding, bare structural silhouettes, and dormant terminal buds.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, delivery vans, pedestrians, temporary traffic cones, signage, and photogrammetric mesh artifacts are fully expunged.
