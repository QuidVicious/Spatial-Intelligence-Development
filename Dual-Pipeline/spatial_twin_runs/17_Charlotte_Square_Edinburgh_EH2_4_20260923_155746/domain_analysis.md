# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sandstone and volcanic tuff bedrock. Superstructure composed of fine-grained pale cream-to-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstone ashlar, bedded with non-hydraulic lime mortar. Stable urban ground with historic subterranean drainage and basement lightwells.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian Neoclassical unified palatial terrace designed by Robert Adam, flanked around the central West Register House (formerly St George's Church, Robert Reid). Three-storey plus basement and attic residential bays with rusticated ground floors and ashlar upper levels. Central monument features a colossal tetrastyle Ionic portico, stepped attic base, cylindrical drum with Corinthian pilasters, and a ribbed hemispherical cupola. Fenestration consists of timber 12-pane and 6-over-6 sash-and-case windows with ashlar architraves. True plumb vertical load-bearing lines and rectified straight rooflines.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Droved and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar with historic coal-smoke carbon patination and rain-washed reveals. Verdigris-oxidized standing-seam copper dome with cast-lead roof flashings. Scotch slate roofing with dressed stone chimney stacks. Cast-iron perimeter spearhead railings and Caithness stone flags along pedestrian walkways; fine-aggregate asphalt roadbed with painted thermoplastic markings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Bordering parkland canopy featuring mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Late September phenology: full canopy structure with early chlorosis, yellowing leaf margins, and minor localized leaf drop along lower branches.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All deformed photogrammetry vehicle artifacts, pedestrian presence, traffic cones, and temporary municipal street furniture are removed. Only static architectural fabric, stone kerbs, iron railings, permanent paving, and mature trees remain.
