A 35mm photograph captured at eye-level from the water's edge looking northeast along the curving shoreline of Indianola, Washington, where prominent two-storey coastal residences rising to 8.6 meters elevation perch along the crest of a dramatic, light-tan glaciofluvial bluff. The sheer sandy and gravel-flecked cliff face reveals layered glacial till strata, interspersed with patches of coastal brush and creeping salal, plunging down to a sweeping curved foreshore of smooth grey cobbles, coarse sand, and scattered sun-bleached driftwood logs. Above the cliff, the 2-storey timber-clad homes feature warm cedar shingles, expansive ocean-facing windows, cantilevered sun decks, and pitched gabled roofs, nestled tightly beneath a dense backdrop of tall, spire-like Douglas firs, western redcedars, and spreading bigleaf maples. Bathed in the low, rich amber raking light of Golden Hour at 3300K, the western-facing slopes and tree canopies catch brilliant golden highlights, casting soft, elongated shadows toward the water, while the calm, gently rippling sea of Puget Sound reflects deep navy and shimmering warm amber hues against the distant hazy silhouette of the Cascade Range across the horizon. Shot on Kodak Portra 400, crisp textural sharpness on sand, pine needles, and weathered bluff soil.
