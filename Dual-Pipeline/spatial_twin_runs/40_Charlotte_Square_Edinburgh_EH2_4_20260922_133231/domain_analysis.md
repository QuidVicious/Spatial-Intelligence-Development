# Spatial Domain Analysis: 40 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Wardie Shales and sandstone of the Inverclyde Group) capped by glacial till. Facades and monuments utilize local Carboniferous Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone, set in traditional hot-mixed non-hydraulic lime mortar. Basement areas are underlain by well-drained glacial drift over fractured sandstone.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical civic square and church (West Register House, formerly St George's Church) terminated by Robert Adam's unified palace-fronted terrace design, completed by Robert Reid. Central church features a tetrastyle Ionic portico supporting a stepped podium, circular peristyle drum, copper dome, and cupola lantern. Flanking terraces comprise 3-storey plus basement and attic ashlar townhouses with rusticated ground floors, projecting pavilion bays with attached colonnades, pitched slate roofs with ashlar chimney stacks, and regular 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case fenestration. Central garden features the equestrian Prince Albert Memorial on a multi-tiered polished granite pedestal.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House: Fine-dressed Craigleith ashlar sandstone; verdigris-patinated oxidized copper dome and lantern cupola; cast-lead roof flashings; cast-bronze commemorative sculpture.
- Terraces: Ashlar sandstone upper storeys over rusticated Craigleith basement and ground floors; dark grey Scottish Ballachulish slates; cast-iron balcony railings and area railings.
- Memorial: Cast bronze statuary on a carved pink Peterhead granite plinth with inset bronze relief panels.
- Weathering: Localized atmospheric coal-smoke crusts, rain-wash leaching on upper string courses, and light biological greening on northern sandstone basements.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Charlotte Square Gardens canopy is dominated by mature *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), interspersed with *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm). Late September phenology shows early senescent canopy chlorosis, partial foliar yellowing, and minor early autumn leaf thinning across upper crowns over mown *Festuca-Agrostis* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, traffic, pedestrians, dumpsters, temporary signage, and photogrammetry artifacts are fully expunged. Static architectural fabric, permanent wrought-iron perimeter railings, dressed stone kerbstones, and mature trees are preserved.
