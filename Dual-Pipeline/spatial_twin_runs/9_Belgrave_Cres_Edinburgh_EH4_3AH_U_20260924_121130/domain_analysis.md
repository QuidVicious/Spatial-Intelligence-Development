* **Lithics:** Craigleith buff-grey quartzose sandstone ashlar for main ashlar facades; secondary Hailes laminated sandstone rubble for boundary walls; Scottish metamorphic roofing slate.
* **Mortar & Drainage:** Non-hydraulic slaked lime and coarse river sand mortar; alluvial clay-silt subsoil grading into Water of Leith drainage basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
* **Residential Facades (Left):** Victorian Classical terrace, three storeys over sunken basement. Stugged ashlar basement courses, polished ashlar upper levels, projecting canted bay windows, molded architraves, projecting cornices, and timber-framed sash-and-case fenestration.
* **Ecclesiastical Structure (Center):** Gothic Revival parish church with multi-stage square ashlar tower, corner pinnacles, blind tracery parapets, lancet windows, and planar nave buttresses.
* **Boundary & Infrastructure:** Low coursed-rubble retaining walls with weathered coping stones; rolled asphalt lane surface flanked by dressed whinstone and granite kerbstones.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina

* **Civil Ground Surfaces:** Coarse bituminous asphalt roadway, riven stone flags along footway margins, and wrought-iron boundary railings.
