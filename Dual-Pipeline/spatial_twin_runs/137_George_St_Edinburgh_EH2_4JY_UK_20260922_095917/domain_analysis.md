# Spatial Domain Analysis: 137 George St, Edinburgh EH2 4JY, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock**: Lower Carboniferous sedimentary sequence (Inverclyde Group / Calders Member sandstones and siltstones) overlying volcanic basalt sills.
- **Lithics**: Fine-to-medium-grained pale buff-grey Craigleith and Hailes carboniferous sandstone; Scotch slate roofing; bronze and polished Peterhead granite for the central memorial plinth.
- **Mortar & Hydrology**: Non-hydraulic slaked lime and coarse river sand mortars; free-draining urban loam over glacial till.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **West Register House (Centre)**: Neoclassical monumental civic church conversion (Robert Adam design, executed by Robert Reid, 1811–1814). Symmetrical three-bay temple-front portico with colossal Ionic columns, entablature, pediment, attic tier, cylindrical drum, and copper dome.
- **Flanking Terraces (North and South West Pavilions)**: 3-storey plus attic and rusticated basement Georgian palace-front townhouses. Flemish-derived paired end pavilions, continuous string courses, balustraded parapets, 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 timber sash-and-case fenestration.
- **Distant Skyline**: Triple stone spires of St Mary's Episcopal Cathedral (Gothic Revival, central crossing tower and twin western spires) and civic spires/chimney stacks.
- **Memorial**: Central Prince Albert Equestrian Monument (Sir John Steell, 1876); bronze equestrian figure atop stepped polished red granite pedestal with bas-relief panels.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Monumental & Domestic Masonry**: Ashlar Craigleith sandstone with fine droved and polished dressing; rusticated ashlar at ground tiers; localized soot crusts, salt leaching, and grey-black atmospheric patina.
- **Dome**: Oxidized standing-seam verdigris copper sheeting with gilded cross/finial.
- **Roofing**: Scottish West Highland and Ballachulish grey-black slates, lead flashing, stone chimney stacks with ceramic buff-terracotta pots.
- **Ground & Enclosure**: Central manicured perennial ryegrass lawn, perimeter gravel/asphalt carriage pathways, stone kerbstones, wrought-iron perimeter railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Species**: *Ulmus glabra* (Wych elm), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common lime), and *Prunus spinosa* perimeter shrubs.
- **Phenology (Late December)**: Complete winter dormancy; 100% defoliated bare canopies; exposed complex fractal branch architecture, dark damp bark, and dormant winter turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, moving traffic, parked automobiles, pedestrians, roadworks, and modern refuse bins are excluded. Fixed historical street furniture, cast-iron railings, kerbstones, and static monument statuary remain intact.
