# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, predominantly calciferous sandstones interbedded with siltstone and basaltic volcanic intrusions. Primary structural lithics consist of fine-to-medium-grained Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartz-arenite sandstones. Subterranean hydrological run-off drains south-west toward the palaeo-lacustrine basin of the drained Nor' Loch. Mortars are historic hot-mixed, non-hydraulic lime and sand matrices with localized Portland cement repointing on mid-to-late 20th-century infills.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Central public garden square centered on the 1821 Melville Monument: a fluted Roman Doric sandstone column atop a multi-tiered rusticated ashlar pedestal, surmounted by a heroic stone figure. Perimeter block layout features Georgian classical townhouses (4 to 5 storeys), Victorian commercial palazzi, and late-20th-century corporate office blocks (5 to 7 storeys). Rectified orthogonal geometry: plumb vertical masonry walls, straight cornices, rusticated ashlar basements, vertical timber 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 sash-and-case fenestration grids, modern flat-panel curtain-wall glazing, and mansard slate roofs with lead-dressed dormers.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Historic Envelopes:** Craigleith and Hailes sandstone ashlar facades, rusticated ground courses, Ballachulish grey-black roofing slates, lead flashing, wrought-iron perimeter boundary railings.
- **Modern Infill:** Anodized aluminium mullions, clear float glass curtain walls, precast Portland stone cladding panels, ribbed bronze spandrel panels.
- **Paving & Ground:** Caithness flagstone paving, dressed granite kerb stones, crushed gravel radial paths, copper-domed garden pavilion with oxidized verdigris standing seams, steel inset tram rails embedded in brushed asphalt.
- **Weathering:** Localized atmospheric coal-smoke crusts on protected friezes, rain-washed clean ashlar on exposed upper stories, minor biological greening at ground basements.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Perimeter and interior parkland planting consists of mature *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), *Tilia cordata* (small-leaved lime), and *Betula pendula* (silver birch). Late-September phenology: dense, mature canopies exhibiting early autumn senescent chlorosis, fading from deep olive green to muted ochre and pale yellow along outer leaf margins, with intact foliage volume and visible branch frameworks beneath.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient entities—including motor vehicles, buses, trams, pedestrians, temporary traffic cones, signage, scaffolding sheeting, and waste bins—are completely expunged. Only static civic architecture, fixed ironwork, streetlamps, paved surfaces, tram tracks, and living botanical specimens remain.
