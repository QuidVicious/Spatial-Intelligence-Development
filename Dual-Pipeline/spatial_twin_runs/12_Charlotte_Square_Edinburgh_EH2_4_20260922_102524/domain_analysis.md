# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean strata of Lower Carboniferous sandstone and siltstone (Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups) overlain by compacted glacial lodgement till. Primary structural masonry comprises fine-grained, pale buff-to-grey quartz-arenite sandstone quarried from local Craigleith and Hailes deposits. Bedding courses bound with traditional hydraulic lime and sharp silica sand mortars.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban square composition dominated by West Register House (formerly St George's Church). Central monument features a monumental tetrastyle portico, engaged giant order colonnade, square clock-stage attic, and a tiered cylindrical drum supporting a ribbed hemispherical dome with terminal cupola lantern. Flanking Robert Adam-designed terraces form unified multi-bay, three-storey compositions over rusticated basements, featuring planar ashlar facades, string courses, mutule cornices, continuous balustraded parapets, regular 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case fenestration, and steep slate mansards with robust chimney stacks. Central foreground retains the multi-tiered Prince Albert equestrian monument.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House & Terraces: Sawn Craigleith sandstone ashlar; channelled rustication at ground tier, smooth ashlar above; oxidised verdigris copper cladding on dome; dark Scottish Ballachulish slate roofs; cast-iron rainwater goods. Localised gypsum-carbon crusting in sheltered mouldings with rain-washed facade panels.
- Prince Albert Memorial: Cast bronze statuary on stepped pink Peterhead granite and sandstone plinth.
- Civil Surfacing: Manicured parkland turf, dressed whinstone and granite kerbstones, asphalt road ring, cast-iron perimeter spear-head railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban parkland specimens of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). December winter phenology: completely defoliated, displaying bare dendritic branch architectures, exposed dark bark trunks, dormant terminal buds, and dormant *Lolium perenne* turf groundcover.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicular traffic, parked cars, pedestrians, modern bins, and temporary street clutter completely expunged. Static permanent railings, stone kerbs, plinths, and architectural structures preserved.
