# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Inverleith Formation (sandstones, siltstones, mudstones) overlain by dense Quaternary glacial till. Building fabric utilizes pale buff-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstone, characterized by high-silica quartz matrix. Bedding mortar is non-hydraulic hot-mixed lime with local aggregate; subterranean drainage manages high moisture retention in compacted till subsoil.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban terrace and civic monumentality (Robert Adam design executed by Robert Reid, completed 1814). Rectified vertical planes throughout:
- Center background features West Register House (former St. George's Church): monumental square base with Ionic portico, stepped podium, circular stone drum punctuated by round-arched niches, crowned by a hemispherical copper dome and stone lantern.
- Attached flanking terraces: 3-storey over raised basement with attic storeys. Channelled rustication on ground floors, smooth Craigleith ashlar upper storeys, continuous stringcourses, modillion cornices, and balustraded roof parapets. 
- Fenestration: rectified 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 double-hung timber sash-and-case windows set in regular grid bays with clean ashlar architraves.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Buff-blonde Craigleith ashlar sandstone displaying characteristic soot-patination, rain-wash tracks, and localized micro-crusting along projecting cornices.
- Dome: Green-patinated oxidized sheet copper with standing seams over timber substructure.
- Roofs: Grey-black Ballachulish slate in diminishing courses behind sandstone balustrades; ashlar chimney stacks with terracotta pots.
- Ground: Mastic asphalt roadway flanked by split Caithness stone flag footways and whinstone/granite kerbing. Heavy cast-iron spearhead railings and urn finials fronting basement lightwells.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Charlotte Square Garden perimeter retains mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Canopy phenology for late September: full-volume crowns showing early autumn senescence, localized chlorosis with margins yellowing, minimal leaf-drop, exposing rough grey-brown fissured bark.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh artifacts, distorted vehicles, pedestrians, dumpsters, and transient barriers eliminated. Retains structural granite kerbs, cast-iron railings, fixed heritage lamp standards, and pristine botanical framing.
