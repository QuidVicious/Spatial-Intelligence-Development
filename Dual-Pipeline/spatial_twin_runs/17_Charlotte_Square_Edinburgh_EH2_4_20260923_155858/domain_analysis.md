# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean strata comprise Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock with volcanic basalt intrusions of the Lothian formation. Facade masonry consists of pale buff-to-grey Craigleith quartz-arenite sandstone ashlar over Hailes sandstone rubble footings. Mortar is historic hot-mixed hydraulic lime with graded quartz aggregate.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian Neo-Classical civic and residential terrace composition (Robert Adam design executed by Robert Reid, early 19th century). Dominated by West Register House (former St George's Church) featuring a colossal Ionic portico, multi-tiered square clock base, circular columnar drum, and high copper dome. Adjoining unified terraces comprise three-storey plus attic and sunken basement profiles, characterized by ground-level channelled ashlar rustication, smooth dressed upper ashlar, regular 6-over-6 timber sash fenestration, projecting string courses, mutule cornices, and parapet balustrades.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith sandstone ashlar with industrial coal-smoke patina and localized gypsum crusting.
- Dome: Standing-seam copper sheet with oxidized green verdigris patination.
- Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish grey slate.
- Civil Ironwork: Black-painted cast iron spearhead railings and overthrow lamps.
- Ground: Bituminous road surface, split Caithness flagstone pavements, dressed whinstone kerbs.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Bordering parkland features mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Late September phenology: full canopy volumes exhibiting early autumn senescence, chlorotic margin yellowing, and minor crown defoliation.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry artifacts, low-poly vehicle volumes, street clutter, and transient pedestrian forms removed. Structural lines rectified to true plumb vertical.
