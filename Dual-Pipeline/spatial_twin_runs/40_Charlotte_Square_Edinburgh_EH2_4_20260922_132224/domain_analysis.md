# Spatial Domain Analysis: 40 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sandstones and siltstones of the Strathclyde Group, overlain by dense, stiff Devensian glacial till.
- Lithic Fabric: Primary walling constructed of fine-to-medium-grained Carboniferous Craigleith quartzose sandstone; central monument plinth of polished red Peterhead granite.
- Mortar Chemistry: Traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic Scottish lime mortars prepared with sharp river sand.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- West Register House (Former St George's Church): Symmetrical Neoclassical civic monumentality; hexastyle portico base supporting a square attic, circular colonnaded peristyle drum, and ribbed copper-clad hemispherical dome capped with a gilded lantern and ball finial.
- Flanking Terraces (Robert Adam design): 3-storey plus attic and basement Georgian townhouse ranges; channelled rusticated ground floor with round-headed window reveals, smooth ashlar upper storeys with giant order engaged columns and pilasters, continuous entablatures, and mansard slate roofs punctuated by dormers and shared ashlar chimney stacks.
- Prince Albert Memorial: Central pedestal monument featuring stepped rusticated plinth, dressed granite socle, and equestrian bronze statuary with corner bronze groupings.
- Fenestration: Vertical 6-over-6 and 8-over-8 timber-framed double-hung sash-and-case windows set in clean ashlar reveals.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Buff-grey Craigleith sandstone ashlar with fine droved and polished tooling; channels on ground-floor rustication; localized coal-smoke particulate encrustation in sheltered mouldings.
- Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish blue-grey roofing slates with lead ridge rolls, zinc flashings, and verdigris-oxidized copper sheet dome skin.
- Monument: Dark patinated cast bronze equestrian figure and bronze relief plaques mounted on red Peterhead granite with honed finishes.
- Ground Surfaces: Mown turf with herbaceous borders, surrounded by dressed granite kerbstones and historic cast-iron spear-topped perimeter railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Botanical Composition: Mixed urban parkland featuring *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore maple), *Tilia × europaea* (Common lime), and *Betula pendula* (Silver birch).
- Phenology (Late September): Early autumn chlorosis and foliage senescence; partial crown thinning, yellowing canopy margins, with intact structural branching.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, parked cars, pedestrians, traffic barriers, and temporary civil clutter are entirely removed. Dressed stone kerbs, iron railings, streetlamps, architectural monuments, and mature botanicals are fully retained.
