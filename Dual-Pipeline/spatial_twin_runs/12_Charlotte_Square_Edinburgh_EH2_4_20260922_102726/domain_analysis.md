# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary sandstones and mudstones overlain by glacial till.
- Lithic extraction: Pale blonde to buff Craigleith and Hailes carboniferous sandstone; dressed ashlar blocks.
- Statue plinth: Polished pink Peterhead granite base courses with tooled margins.
- Mortar: Historic hydraulic lime and sharp silica sand pointing.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Landmark center: Former St George's Church (West Register House), designed by Robert Adam and revised by Robert Reid (1811–1814). Neoclassical Greek-cross plan with a giant Roman Ionic tetrastyle portico, surmounted by a stepped square attic, a peristyle drum with Corinthian colonnade, and a ribbed copper-clad dome topped with a lantern and gilded cross [1].
- Flanking terraces: 3-storey plus attic and basement neoclassical Georgian townhouses, dressed Craigleith ashlar, rusticated ground floors, regular 3-bay modules with timber 12-pane sash-and-case fenestration, projecting eaves cornices, and pitched Scottish slate roofs with ashlar chimney stacks.
- Central monument: Equestrian monument to Prince Albert (1876, Sir John Steell) comprising a cast bronze equestrian bronze group atop a stepped pink granite pedestal with inset bronze relief panels [2].
- Planar rectification: Verticals plumbed true; facade planes and continuous string courses aligned; photogrammetric mesh artifacts resolved to planar masonry surfaces.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Fine-grained, cross-bedded blonde sandstone ashlar with micro-tooled finish; authentic surface sulfur-soot incrustation in sheltered reveals and rain-washed upper moldings.
- Dome: Standing-seam copper sheet exhibiting aged verdigris patina.
- Roofs: Ballachulish blue-grey slate with lead flashings, ridges, and valleys.
- Monument: Oxidized cast bronze with localized green copper-carbonate weathering on polished pink granite plinth [2].
- Ground: Well-maintained perennial rye and fescue winter lawn enclosed by cast-iron perimeter railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Deciduous tree species: *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
- Phenology (Late December): Fully dormant, complete winter defoliation; bare structural branch silhouettes with dark, damp bark; dormant terminal buds.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- All transient motor vehicles, peripheral road traffic, parked cars, pedestrians, and temporary maintenance fixtures are removed.
- Ground topography rectified to smooth, continuous manicured lawn and clean whinstone kerbstones.
