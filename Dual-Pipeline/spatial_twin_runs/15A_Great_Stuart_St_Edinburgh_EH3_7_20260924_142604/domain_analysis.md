# Spatial Domain Analysis: 15A Great Stuart St, Edinburgh EH3 7TP, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Wardie Shales and Sandstones).
- Lithic Fabric: Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey carboniferous sandstone ashlar; hydraulic lime mortar pointing; Ballachulish grey-black metamorphic slate roofing.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Typology: Edinburgh New Town Neoclassical Georgian residential terrace (James Gillespie Graham / Moray Estate plan).
- Massing: 3 storeys plus attic storey with dormers and rusticated basement lightwell.
- Rectification: True vertical plumb-line facades, planar sandstone ashlar courses, aligned 12-pane (6-over-6) and 4-over-4 timber sash-and-case fenestration, continuous moulded eaves cornice, ashlar chimney stacks with ceramic pots.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facade: Finely dressed droved and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar blocks with fine lime mortar joints.
- Weathering: Selective atmospheric soot crusting under projecting cornices, rain-washed facade panels, mild localized iron oxide staining.
- Ground/Enclosure: Cast iron spear-head railings over dressed sandstone plinths; Caithness flagstone paving and whinstone (basalt/dolerite) kerbs.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Specimen: *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore).
- Phenology (Late September): Mature dense crowns displaying early autumn foliar chlorosis, minor leaf crisping, and isolated canopy thinning.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Complete elimination of all transient human subjects, modern vehicles, traffic infrastructure, bins, and temporary urban clutter.
