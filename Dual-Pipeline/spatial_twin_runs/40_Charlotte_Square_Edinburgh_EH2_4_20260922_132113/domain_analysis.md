# Spatial Domain Analysis: 40 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock**: Carboniferous sedimentary strata (sandstone, siltstone, oil-shale) overlying intrusive volcanic basalt and dolerite sills of the Midland Valley.
- **Lithics**: Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone ashlar; Peterhead pink and grey granite steps and monument plinths.
- **Mortar & Ground**: Hydraulic lime-sand mortar with fine aggregate; free-draining urban topsoil over glacial till (boulder clay).

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Central Monumental Structure**: West Register House (former St George's Church, Robert Reid / Robert Adam); Neoclassical Greek-cross plan with four-column Ionic portico, dentiled pediment, cylindrical drum with Corinthian pilasters, and copper-clad hemispherical dome crowned by an octagonal stone lantern.
- **Flanking Terraces**: Charlotte Square west pavilions; 3-storey plus rusticated basement and attic dormers, classical Georgian New Town palace-front facade design, continuous platbands, moulded eaves cornices, and pitched roofs with lead ridges.
- **Fenestration**: Rectilinear grids of 12-pane and 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows set in rebated stone reveals; recessed basement area wells behind cast-iron spearhead railings.
- **Central Monument**: Prince Albert Memorial (Sir John Steell, 1876); bronze equestrian statue group on a stepped polished pink granite and sandstone pedestal with subsidiary bronze bronze corner groupings.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Masonry & Cladding**: Fine-droved and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar; oxidised green verdigris standing-seam copper sheet dome; Welsh/Ballachulish dark grey-blue roofing slate.
- **Metals**: Cast and wrought iron railings in black bitumen paint; patinated statuary bronze with localized copper sulfate greening.
- **Weathering**: Localized coal-smoke sulfurous gypsum crusts in recessed cornice mouldings, rain-washed light buff facade projections, and organic algae staining along plinth bases.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Species Composition**: *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Platanus × acerifolia* (London Plane), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
- **Phenology (Late September)**: Broad-spreading deciduous canopies in late-season deep green foliage displaying early autumnal chlorosis, patchy yellowing on leaf margins, and minor canopy thinning; mown *Lolium perenne* and *Poa pratensis* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Completely purged of all transient motor vehicles, pedestrians, waste bins, temporary barricades, road cones, and ephemeral street furniture. All historic iron railings, stone kerbstones, and mature landscaping are preserved as static fixtures.
