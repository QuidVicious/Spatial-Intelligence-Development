# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Strathclyde Group (sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone) overlain by dense Devensian glacial till.
- Lithic Fabric: Primary facade masonry executed in Carboniferous Craigleith sandstone (pale buff to honey-grey silica-cemented quartz arenite of exceptional structural density); secondary rear walling and foundations incorporate Hailes sandstone and whinstone rubble. Monument plinth constructed from dressed pink/red Peterhead granite.
- Mortar Chemistry: Historic hot-mixed non-hydraulic lime mortar incorporating crushed limestone aggregates and sharp pit sand; modern conservation repointing in hydraulic lime (NHL 2/3.5).
- Hydrogeology: Well-drained gently undulating glacial drift sloping westward towards the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- West Register House (Former St George's Church, 1811–1814, Robert Reid): Neoclassical monumental civic/ecclesiastical structure; colossal tetrastyle Ionic pedimented portico set upon a raised rusticated podium; stepped attic base supporting a cylindrical drum surrounded by an Ionic peristyle colonnade, crowned by an octagonal ribbed hemispherical dome and terminating in a lantern cupola with gilded cross finial.
- Flanking Terraces (Charlotte Square West Range, Robert Adam design): Unified Neoclassical palace-front Georgian townhouses; 3 full storeys over raised rusticated basements with sunken areas, attic storeys set behind balustraded parapets; channelled horizontal rustication at ground floor; timber sash-and-case fenestration arranged in strict vertical alignments (6-over-6 and 8-over-8 panes); pitched mansard roof profiles with Ballachulish slate and dressed ashlar chimney stacks with clay chimney pots.
- Prince Albert Memorial (Sir John Steell, 1876): Monumental equestrian bronze group elevated on a multi-tiered stepped granite pedestal with subsidiary corner bronze sculptural groups and bas-relief bronze narrative friezes.
- Geometry: Absolute planar vertical rectification across all ashlar facades, colonnade shafts, and terraced party-wall parapets.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith sandstone ashlar with fine droved and polished tooling; selective historic carbonaceous coal-smoke encrustations in protected architectural recesses, flutings, and cornice soffits, contrasted by rain-washed pale stone on exposed planar surfaces.
- Dome: Standing-seam sheet copper cladding exhibiting complete, uniform sea-green verdigris patina (basic copper carbonate/sulfate).
- Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish blue-grey slate laid in diminishing courses, lead flashings, valleys, and hips.
- Metals: Cast and wrought iron perimeter railings with spearhead finials; oxidized statuary bronze with deep olive-brown and dark malachite green patina.
- Fenestration: Painted white timber joinery framing historic cylinder and float glass panes.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Garden Woodland: Mature temperate urban broadleaf specimens consisting of *Ulmus glabra* (Wych elm), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common lime), and *Aesculus hippocastanum* (Horse chestnut).
- Phenology (Late September): Early autumn transition phase; high canopy retention (approx. 80-85% leaf density) exhibiting peripheral chlorosis, lime foliage turning pale ochre-yellow, sycamore displaying localized tar spot (*Rhytisma acerinum*), horse chestnut displaying leaf-margin bronzing, with initial wind-scattered leaf fall on turf.
- Ground Cover: Fine-mown perennial ryegrass (*Lolium perenne*) and fescue turf forming a continuous green carpet across Charlotte Square Garden.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Complete elimination of all vehicular traffic, buses, vans, parked cars, cyclists, pedestrians, temporary municipal bins, road cones, and transient street signage. Structural cast-iron railings, stone kerbs, historical lampposts, and monuments remain fully preserved.
