# Spatial Domain Analysis: 10 Eton Terrace, Edinburgh EH4 1QD, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Lower Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Lothian Basin, featuring interbedded Calciferous Sandstone Series, siltstones, and doleritic intrusion outcrops along the incised Water of Leith gorge. Architectural and civil lithics comprise pale buff-to-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstone in fine-grained quartzose ashlar, accompanied by igneous whinstone (dolerite) rubble underpinning retaining embankments. Mortar consists of traditional slaked non-hydraulic to hydraulic lime with coarse river sand aggregate, exhibiting deep carbonate leaching and minor lime stalactite efflorescence in damp retaining wall weep holes.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Incised river gorge civil infrastructure and elevated residential terrace boundary fabric. River valley walkway bounded by coursed squared-rubble retaining revetments, dressed sandstone terminal piers, and coped parapets. Upper slopes reveal rear elevations of 19th-century Georgian and early Victorian terraced townhouses constructed in regular ashlar courses with timber sash-and-case fenestration patterns (4-over-4 and 6-over-6 profiles). Pitched timber roofs clad in dark grey Scottish and Westmorland slate with lead-rolled valleys and dressed stone chimney stacks with ceramic pots. All vertical wall planes plumbed to true gravity vertical.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Hewn buff-grey sandstone ashlar and coursed rubble river walls displaying heavy coal-smoke patination, localized biological greening, and moss colonisation along low masonry joints. Cast and wrought iron balustrades coated in dark bituminous paint with localized ferrous oxidation. Compacted crushed whinstone aggregate and asphalt pedestrian path surface edged with hand-dressed whinstone kerbstones. Retaining earth banks surfaced with dark loam and decomposing humic leaf mulch.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Riparian deciduous gorge woodland. Dominated by mature specimens of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), *Fraxinus excelsior* (Ash), and *Alnus glutinosa* (Alder). December winter phenology: fully defoliated canopies with bare, intricate branch architecture, dark damp bark colonized by epiphytic lichens, and a dormant ground layer of leaf litter, ivy (*Hedera helix*), and winter mosses.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient elements including pedestrians, cyclists, waste receptacles, loose litter, and temporary signage are eliminated. Fixed civic fabric, masonry revetments, ironwork, and botanical assets remain intact.
