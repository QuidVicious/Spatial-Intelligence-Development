# Spatial Domain Analysis: West End, Atholl Cres, Edinburgh EH3 8HA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Subterranean foundation set on Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Lower Carboniferous Gullane and Wardie Formations, overlaid by glacial boulder clay (till) and sand/gravel deposits characteristic of the Edinburgh crag-and-tail glacial drift plain.
- Facade Lithics: Predominantly indigenous Craigleith and Hailes pale buff to cool grey quartz-arenite Carboniferous sandstones, known for high silica content and structural durability.
- Mortar Chemistry: Traditional hydraulic and non-hydraulic slaked lime mortars with coarse river sand aggregates, displaying variable micro-fissuring and secondary calcite deposits along joints.
- Drainage & Subterranean Dynamics: Sub-surface water runoff directed along the north-easterly gradient towards the Water of Leith basin, with historic sandstone basements incorporating exterior lightwells and sunken areal vaults.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Typologies: Scottish Georgian, Late Georgian Neoclassical, and Victorian Ecclesiastical / Italianate Renaissance Revival.
- Key Landmark: Former St George's West Church (Charlotte Chapel) featuring a 56-meter Italianate campanile bell tower designed by Sir Robert Rowand Anderson (1879–81) atop the church designed by David Bryce (1866–69), terminating in an octagonal belfry and copper-clad pyramidal spire.
- Distant Skyline Anchor: West Register House (Robert Reid, 1811–1814) terminating Charlotte Square with its prominent classical dome and lantern.
- Urban Geometry & Massing: Consistent 3- to 4-storey plus attic and basement Georgian/Victorian ashlar-faced perimeter blocks flanking Shandwick Place and Maitland Street, characterized by rusticated basements, continuous horizontal stringcourses, dentiled cornices, timber sash-and-case 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 fenestration, and steep slate mansard roofs with prominent stone chimney stacks and clay pots.
- Rectification Protocol: Strict planar vertical rectification of the campanile and Georgian street facades; complete flattening of warped photogrammetric meshes into crisp ashlar planes, straight parapet copings, and rectilinear floor plates.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Ashlar: Fine-to-medium grained grey and honey-buff Craigleith sandstone, finished in droved, polished, and channelled rusticated dressings.
- Weathering & Patina: Atmospheric coal-smoke sulfation crusts and historical soot darkening concentrated beneath cornices, water tables, and window sills, accompanied by biological lichen colonisation (cool greys, pale greens) on north-facing and exposed stone surfaces.
- Spire & Roof Coverings: Verdigris-oxidized pale turquoise-green standing seam copper cladding on the campanile pyramid roof; dark grey Ballachulish and Scottish highland slate roofing across residential tenements; lead flashing along valleys and hips.
- Ground Planes: Dark grey Scottish whinstone (quartz-dolerite) curbs, split-faced granite setts, and modern asphalt roadway carrying twin flush-recessed steel tram rails along the central alignment.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Urban Canopy: Mature broadleaf deciduous trees positioned within private gardens and adjacent crescent enclosures, primarily European Sycamore (*Acer pseudoplatanus*), Wych Elm (*Ulmus glabra*), Common Lime (*Tilia x europaea*), and London Plane (*Platanus x acerifolia*).
- Foliage Structure: Fully resolved organic canopy masses with natural fractal branch structures, detailed leaf clumps, and distinct leafy textures clearly disambiguated from adjacent masonry walls.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Complete purging of all motor vehicles, tram carriages, buses, and transient vehicular movement from Shandwick Place and surrounding junctions.
- Complete removal of all pedestrians, cyclists, temporary sidewalk barriers, wheelie bins, dumpsters, and ephemeral street advertisements.
- Retention of permanent street infrastructure: cast-iron Victorian-style streetlamp standards, municipal steel overhead line poles for trams, stone curb lines, and pavement iron railings.
