# Spatial Domain Analysis: 43 St Andrew Sq, Edinburgh EH2 2AD, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation consists of Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock (sandstones, siltstones, and oil-shales of the Strathclyde Group) overlain by dense Devensian glacial till. Structural masonry across the historic urban perimeter comprises fine-to-medium-grained Carboniferous quartz-arenite Craigleith and Hailes sandstones, bedded in hydraulic lime and sand mortar. Distant topography features the volcanic basaltic neck and teschenite sill intrusions of Arthur's Seat and Salisbury Crags.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Central garden square dominated by the Melville Monument: a 46-metre fluted Roman Doric sandstone column atop a two-tiered rusticated square pedestal, surmounted by a heroic standing stone statue. Perimeter architecture forms a dense continuous street wall:
- Northeast/East: 3- to 5-storey Georgian and Victorian Classical palazzo-style banking halls and townhouses featuring rusticated ashlar basements, piano nobile window pediments, giant-order Corinthian and Ionic pilastrades, entablatures, and balustraded roof parapets punctured by lead-dressed dormers and masonry chimney stacks.
- Southeast/South: Late Victorian French Renaissance/Baronial commercial blocks and railway hotel with a multi-tiered Baronial clock tower; adjacent 20th/21st-century commercial infill featuring vertical structural stone louvres, recessed curtain-wall glazing, and flat plant-screen parapets.
- Background: Arthur's Seat crag profile and the cylindrical Nelson Monument on Calton Hill.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Droved and polished Craigleith blonde sandstone ashlar, rock-faced rusticated sandstone ground floors, weathered carbon-patinated Victorian dressed stonework.
- Modern Infill: Honed light-grey granite piers, anodised aluminium vertical fins, and planar structural glass curtain-wall glazing.
- Roofing: Scottish and Welsh blue-grey roofing slates, lead flashing, copper cupolas, and flat asphalt/zinc decking.
- Ground Surface: Cohesive turf lawns, bonded resin-gravel pedestrian walkways, Caithness stone flagstone pavements, granite kerb stones, and asphalt carriageways with embedded steel tramway rails.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Circumferential urban parkland planted with mature broadleaf specimens:
- *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore): Heavy-limbed, broad rounded canopies with flaking plate-like bark.
- *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime): Dense pyramidal to ovate canopies with characteristic trunk-base epicormic shoots.
- *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm) and *Betula pendula* (Silver Birch): Upright and weeping branching structures forming perimeter shelterbelts.
Ground cover consists of maintained *Lolium perenne* and *Poa pratensis* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All vehicular traffic, trams, pedestrians, temporary banners, construction hoardings, and transient street clutter are eliminated. Fixed civic elements—cast-iron perimeter railings, ornate historic street lanterns, stone kerbing, and tram tracks—remain intact.
