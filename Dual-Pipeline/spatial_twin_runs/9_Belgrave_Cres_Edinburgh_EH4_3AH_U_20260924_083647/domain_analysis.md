# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous Lower Oil-Shale sedimentary strata overlain by glacial till. Superstructure lithics utilize local blonde Craigleith and grey Hailes sandstone. Mortar consists of traditional hot-mixed calcitic hydraulic lime with coarse river sand. Subterranean drainage slopes south towards the Water of Leith valley.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left: Victorian residential terrace, three storeys over raised basement, rectified vertical plane. Ashlar facade with canted bay windows, molded architraves, projecting stone cornices, and timber-framed sash-and-case fenestration. 
Center: Gothic Revival church structure with square multi-stage tower, stepped buttresses, decorative corner pinnacles, crenellated parapets, and pointed-arch lancet windows. Pitched roofs clad in Scottish slate. 
Right/Boundary: Low dressed sandstone dwarf retaining wall with copings flanking the garden perimeter.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Residential Facade: Fine-grained blonde Craigleith ashlar, droved ashlar basement courses, cast-iron rainwater goods, lead flashing.
- Church Fabric: Squared and snecked buff-grey carboniferous sandstone rubble with fine-dressed quoins and ashlar tracery; selective dark carbonaceous atmospheric patina along sheltered recesses.
- Ground Surface: Bituminous road surface bounded by dressed Whinstone kerbs and sandstone flagstone footways.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Bordering parkland belt featuring mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Phenological state for late September: dense full-volume foliage displaying early autumnal chlorosis, lime-green shifts, and patchy leaf margin yellowing.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh artifacts, vehicle polygons, temporary street furniture, and pedestrians fully removed. Street surface, kerbs, stone boundary walls, and tree plantings rectified to static civil infrastructure.
