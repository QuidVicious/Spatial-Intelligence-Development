# Spatial Domain Analysis: 14 Atholl Cres, Edinburgh EH3 8HA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
The underlying terrain is anchored in the Lower Carboniferous Gullane and Wardie Formations of the Strathclyde Group, comprising interbedded pale sandstones, siltstones, and mudstones overlain by glacial till. The primary structural load-bearing lithology throughout the terraces and civic structures is indigenous Craigleith and Hailes quartzose sandstone (fine-to-medium-grained Carboniferous sandstone), alongside select West Lothian buff sandstone. Mortar composition is historic non-hydraulic to semi-hydraulic hot-mixed lime pointing (calcium carbonate matrices with sharp river sand aggregates). Natural subterranean drainage slopes northeastward toward the Water of Leith basin, regulated by subterranean stone culverts and gravity-fed Victorian storm drains.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
A high-angle urban corridor view facing northeast (azimuth ~41°) along the Shandwick Place / Atholl Place axis into Edinburgh's West End and New Town. 
- Foreground Left & Right: Classical Georgian and Victorian ashlar stone residential/commercial terraces (Atholl Crescent and Coates Crescent periphery), rising 3 to 4 storeys plus mansard/attic levels with dormers, rusticated basements, timber sash-and-case fenestration (twelve-pane and four-pane configurations), and prominent arrays of rectangular ashlar chimney stacks with terracotta pots.
- Midground Focal Landmark: St George’s West Church (David Bryce, 1867–1869) on Shandwick Place/Stafford Street, featuring a commanding 56-metre Venetian Romanesque/Baroque campanile tower (Sir Robert Rowand Anderson, 1879–1881) with a four-stage ashlar shaft, clock faces, open octagonal belfry, and a verdigris-oxidized copper pyramidal spire.
- Deep Background: The Neoclassical oxidized copper dome and drum of West Register House (Robert Reid, former St George's Church, Charlotte Square) visible to the left background, leading across the dense slate-roofed planar urban grid toward the distant Firth of Forth horizon.
- Geometry & Alignment: Vertical masonry walls, corner rustication, projecting belt courses, dentilled entablatures, and streetcar/tram track lines aligned in plumb vertical perspective and true horizontal planes.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Dressed blonde-to-honey Craigleith ashlar sandstone, featuring selective historical carbon-soot crusts, micro-spalling along exposed friezes, and atmospheric rain-wash leaching across cornice returns.
- Roofscapes: Scottish Ballachulish and Westmorland blue-grey roofing slate laid in diminishing courses, lead flashings, copper ridge capping, zinc sheet dormer claddings, and weathered verdigris patinas across church spires and dome cupolas.
- Openings: Painted white and dark wood-framed sliding sash windows with clear float/crown glass; black cast-iron balcony railings and basement area guardrails with lead-caulked copings.
- Street Level: Dense bitumen roadway surface, polished granite sett bands, precision-cut Whinstone and granite kerbstones, and embedded double-steel tram tracks running parallel down the central carriageway.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Urban municipal and crescent park planting includes mature Broadleaf deciduous specimens:
- European Sycamore (*Acer pseudoplatanus*)
- Wych Elm (*Ulmus glabra*)
- English Oak (*Quercus robur*)
- Common Lime (*Tilia x europaea*)
Canopies exhibit rich, naturalized leafy branching forms flanking Atholl Crescent gardens in the lower foreground corners, clearly articulated as organic biomass separate from adjacent stone facades.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicular traffic (automobiles, delivery vans, trams, and buses), pedestrian crowds, trash bins, construction barricades, and temporary promotional banners have been completely removed. Permanent urban fixtures—including cast-iron Victorian-style lamp columns, directional road markings, steel tram rails, stone bollards, and boundary iron railings—are preserved with immaculate documentary clarity.
