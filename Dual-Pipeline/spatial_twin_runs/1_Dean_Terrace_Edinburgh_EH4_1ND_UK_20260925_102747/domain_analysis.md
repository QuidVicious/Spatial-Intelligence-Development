# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 Dean Terrace, Edinburgh EH4 1ND, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock overlain by glacial till. Structural masonry derived from local Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartz-arenite sandstone. Carriageway paved in igneous quartz-dolerite (whinstone) setts. Lime mortar bedding with natural hydraulic properties; gravity drainage trending downhill toward the Water of Leith.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian Neoclassical multi-storey residential tenements (3-4 storeys over sunken basement lightwells). Continuous horizontal ashlar facades with channelled rustication at ground level, smooth ashlar above, and classical moulded eaves cornices. Rectilinear fenestration grid of 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 timber sash-and-case windows. Pitched timber roofs clad in grey-black Scotch slate, punctuated by substantial ashlar chimney stacks with terracotta pots. Perimeter cast-iron spearhead railings enclosing sunken basement areas.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Fine-grained blonde and olive-buff Craigleith sandstone ashlar with localized coal-smoke carbon crusting in sheltered mouldings.
- Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish slate with lead flashing, valleys, and ridges.
- Ground Plane: Whinstone sett carriageway with Caithness flagstone footways and dressed granite kerbstones.
- Ironwork: Black-painted wrought and cast-iron balustrades and basement railings.
- Fenestration: Painted white timber joinery with clear float glass glazing.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Bordering urban canopy comprising *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved Lime). Late December mid-winter phenology: complete seasonal defoliation, fully exposed branch architecture, dormant terminal buds, and bare grey-brown bark.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, parked cars, pedestrians, waste bins, and temporary street clutter removed. Structural stone kerbs, cobbled roadway, perimeter ironwork, fixed lanterns, and dormant botanical elements remain strictly preserved.
