# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Early Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Gullane Formation) overlying basaltic volcanic intrusions. 
Lithics: Primary structural masonry of Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey carboniferous sandstone. Hydraulic lime and calcitic bedding mortar.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Urban Fabric: Symmetrical Georgian New Town layout centered on St Andrew Square, transitioning into linear George Street corridor.
Central Feature: Melville Monument; 45-meter fluted Roman Doric column on stepped square pedestal supporting colossal standing stone statuary.
Massing & Typologies: 
- 4-to-6-storey classical Georgian and Victorian perimeter blocks; ashlar rustication on ground storeys, molded architraves, projecting cornices.
- Modernist and late-20th-century infill commercial blocks featuring gridded concrete, polished stone panels, and curtain-wall modules.
- Roofscapes: Pitched Scotch slate roofs with lead-rolled hips, dormer windows, stone parapets, and regular arrays of dressed ashlar chimney stacks.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith sandstone ashlar with fine droved and polished dressing; variable historic coal-smoke patination and atmospheric sooting.
- Fenestration: Recessed multi-pane timber sash-and-case windows in 6-over-6 and 8-over-8 configurations; modern commercial units with anodized aluminum frames.
- Paving & Hardscape: Whinstone and granite setts, sawn sandstone slab pavements, cast-iron perimeter railings, and integrated steel tram rails set into road asphalt.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Park Enclosure: *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) ringing the central perimeter; isolated *Betula pendula* specimens.
Phenology (Late September): Fully developed canopies displaying early-stage autumn senescence, characterized by chlorotic yellowing margins and partial foliar browning across the crowns. Central circular lawn composed of perennial rye-grass (*Lolium perenne*).

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient elements excluded: zero vehicular traffic, red buses, trams, pedestrians, temporary scaffolding wraps, or street-level rubbish. All permanent stone kerbs, trackbeds, fixed lanterns, and bronze monuments retained.
