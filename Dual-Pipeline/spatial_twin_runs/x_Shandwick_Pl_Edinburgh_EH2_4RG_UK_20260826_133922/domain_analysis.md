## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Ashlar & Rusticated Sandstone**: Dressed Craigleith/Hailes buff-grey ashlar facades with finely tooled margins, contrasting with heavier rustication on ground floors and corner pilasters.
- **Roofing & Flashings**: Scottish Ballachulish and West Highland dark blue-grey metamorphic roofing slates laid in diminishing courses, secured with copper nails, and trimmed with rolled lead ridging, valley gutters, and lead-clad dormer cheeks.
- **Campanile Spire**: Heavy verdigris copper sheet cladding oxidized to a distinct pale celadon/sea-green patina on the pyramidal cap of the St George's West tower.
- **Environmental Weathering & Climate Pathology**:
  - Deep carbonaceous soot crusting (historic coal smoke deposition) concentrated in sheltered under-cornices, window reveals, and pediment recesses.
  - Natural rainwater washing paths creating vertical silver-buff wash streaks on exposed ashlar faces.
  - Subdued biological micro-algal growth and localized lichen dusting along damper north- and west-facing horizontal stone cornices and parapet copings.
