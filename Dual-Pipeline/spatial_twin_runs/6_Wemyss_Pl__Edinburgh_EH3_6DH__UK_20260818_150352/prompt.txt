Looking north-northwest down the gentle slope of Darnaway Street toward the distant hills and the Firth of Forth horizon, the composition frames flanking palatial Craigleith pale buff sandstone townhouses detailed with crisp ground-floor channelled rustication, precise belt courses, and 12-pane timber sash-and-case windows. The carriageway consists of wet, textured dark grey whinstone sett paving lined with Caithness flagstone pavements and cast-iron drain covers, while parked modern vehicles line the curb under the dappled shade of Queen Street Gardens' mature deciduous canopy to the right. Clear, temperate afternoon daylight at 5400K casts soft, well-defined shadows along the building facades, highlighting the rich weathered patina and soot-streaked mineral grain of the Scottish masonry without artificial distortion. Shot on Kodak Portra 400, 35mm focal length, f/8 aperture, razor-sharp architectural focus and rectilinear geometry across the entire frame.
