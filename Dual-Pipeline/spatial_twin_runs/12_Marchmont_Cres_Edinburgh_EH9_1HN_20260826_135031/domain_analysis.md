# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Marchmont Cres, Edinburgh EH9 1HN, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
The subterranean geology of Marchmont rests on the Lower Carboniferous Gullane Formation and sedimentary sequences of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, underlain by glacial till over Devonian sandstone and volcanic basalt intrusions characteristic of the Edinburgh basin. The primary building envelope is constructed from indigenous Scottish Carboniferous blond and buff-grey sandstones, historically quarried from the Hailes, Craigleith, and Clunevar quarries. Ground masonry shows a predominance of course-grained quartz-rich arenite set in traditional lime-based mortar (calcium hydroxide with sharp river sand aggregate), providing natural breathability and moisture transversion through continuous wet-dry atmospheric cycles. Subterranean moisture is directed through historic brick-lined drainage runs and vaulted sub-pavements typical of late Victorian suburban development.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
The scene captures a dense urban intersection centered on Marchmont Crescent curving toward Warrender Park and Spottiswoode Street. Typology consists entirely of late Victorian (c. 1876–1890) Scots Baronial and 17th-century Scottish vernacular residential tenement blocks.
- **Centre & Curved Frontages:** 4-storey plus attic/mansard levels (approx. 14–16m to wall-head, 18m to ridge lines) featuring full-height canted bay windows, conical-roofed corner bartizans/tourelles, crow-stepped and pedimented gables, corbelled corner transitions, and elaborate dormer projections.
- **Fenestration & Vertical Plumb:** Rhythms of regular timber-framed 1-over-1 and multi-pane sash and case windows set deeply into chamfered ashlar reveals. Ground floor features traditional recessed timber shopfronts with intact fascia signbands and cast-iron vent grilles.
- **Roofscape:** Steeply pitched Scottish blue-grey slate roofs with lead ridges, valley flashings, zinc-capped mansard flat decks, glazed rooflights, and massive squared ashlar mutual chimney stacks bearing cylindrical terracotta chimney pots.
- **Anti-Warp Planar Rectification:** All tenement facades, bay window stacks, and corner towers are restored to absolute plumb verticality, correcting mesh melt and camera pitch distortions. Balcony courses, string courses, and eaves lines form uninterrupted horizontal planes.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Ashlar & Snecked Rubble:** Polished and fine-tooled ashlar sandstone quoins, window dressings, corbels, and lintels contrasting against rock-faced squared snecked sandstone walling.
- **Roofing & Flashings:** Scotch slate with natural variable thickness and blue-grey slate tones, punctuated by rolled lead flashing, cast-iron rainwater downpipes, and lead rainwater heads.
- **Climate-Adaptive Pathology (Historic Temperate Urban Weathering):** Deep carbonaceous atmospheric deposits and historic coal-soot gypsum crusting in the recessed undercrofts of corbels, cornices, and window pediments; vertical rainwater runoff staining and differential pale wash along exposed ashlar stringers; natural golden-buff oxidization and localized salt leaching on north-facing plinth zones.
- **Pavements & Roadway:** Traditional Caithness flagstone footpaths bordered by whinstone (basalt) and granite kerbing, meeting fine-grained asphalt carriageways with weathered painted civil lane markings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Street-level and rear communal garden greenery consists of mature deciduous urban canopy trees:
- **Species:** Sycamore (*Acer pseudoplatanus*), London Plane (*Platanus x acerifolia*), Common Lime (*Tilia x europaea*), and native Birch (*Betula pendula*).
- **Botanical Detailing:** Rich, organic, multi-layered broadleaf foliage with distinct branch geometry, leaf clusters, and natural sunlight percolation, fully disambiguated from adjacent masonry.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient urban noise has been eliminated:
- **Removed:** All motor vehicles (parked and moving cars, delivery vans), pedestrians, cyclists, temporary wheelie bins, plastic street clutter, and temporary paper notices.
- **Retained:** Fixed Victorian cast-iron perimeter railings atop dwarf sandstone boundary walls, municipal street signage, cast-iron bollards, granite kerbstones, and fixed road surface civil markings.
