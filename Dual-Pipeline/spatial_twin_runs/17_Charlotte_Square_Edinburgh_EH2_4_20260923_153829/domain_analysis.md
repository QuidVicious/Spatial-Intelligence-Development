# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Gullane Formation / Lower Oil-Shale Group) comprising intercalated sandstones, siltstones, and mudstones underlying the Edinburgh New Town plateau.
- Masonry Lithics: Pale cream-to-buff, fine-to-medium grained Carboniferous quartz-arenite sandstone quarried predominantly from Craigleith Quarry (distinguished by high silica matrix and structural longevity), supplemented with darker laminated sandstone from Hailes Quarry.
- Memorial Plinth: Polished igneous pink Peterhead granite base with dressed Carboniferous freestone footing.
- Mortar & Foundations: Historic non-hydraulic slaked lime mortars compounded with sharp crushed quartz sand; dry-laid rubble foundation footings bearing directly onto dense glacial till over sedimentary bedrock.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- West Register House (Former St George's Church): Neoclassical monumental civic architecture designed by Robert Reid (adapting Robert Adam's 1791 scheme); dominant central copper-sheathed dome over a drum surrounded by a Corinthian colonnade, surmounting a rusticated ground floor with a colossal tetrastyle portico carrying a full classical entablature and triangular pediment.
- Residential Perimeter: Robert Adam designed Neoclassical unified palace-front terraces; 3-storey plus attic and rusticated basement configurations; symmetrical terminal and central pavilions with attached Corinthian/Ionic pilasters; Scottish Ballachulish slate mansard and pitched roof planes; aligned ashlar chimney stacks with terracotta pots; 6-over-6 and 12-pane timber sash-and-case fenestration grids.
- Monument: Central Neo-Renaissance Prince Albert Memorial equestrian statue elevated on a stepped architectural pedestal with flanking subsidiary bronze sculptural groups.
- Rectification: True-plumb vertical facade walls and portico columns; planar alignment of terrace rustication and entablatures; complete restoration of the circular drum and hemispherical dome geometry against photogrammetric mesh distortion.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Finely droved and polished Craigleith ashlar sandstone; channelled ashlar rustication at basement and ground tiers; crisp moulded architraves, modillioned cornices, and pediments.
- Weathering & Patina: Localized historical carbon-particulate crusts embedded within sheltered fluting and under-cornice recesses; rain-washed, clean pale-buff sandstone on exposed westerly vertical surfaces; no structural efflorescence.
- Metalwork: Verdigris oxidized sheet-copper cladding on dome with weathered lead ridge rolls and cast gilt finial; dark-patinated cast bronze equestrian statuary; decorative cast-iron spearhead boundary railings and lampposts coated in satin black paint.
- Roofing: Blue-grey Scottish Ballachulish slate shingles with lead valley gutters, flashing, and ridge caps.
- Ground Surface: Mown *Poa pratensis* and *Festuca rubra* turf lawn intersected by compacted gravel pathways and Yorkstone / Whinstone kerbing.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Temporal Target: 23 September (Early Autumn).
- Species Composition: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Aesculus hippocastanum* (Horse Chestnut), and *Betula pendula* (Silver Birch).
- Canopy Phenology: Dense, high-volume mature canopies entering early autumn senescence; *Tilia × europaea* displaying pale yellow marginal chlorosis; *Aesculus hippocastanum* showing localized leaf-edge browning and early leaflet shedding; *Acer pseudoplatanus* retaining predominantly dark green foliage with sparse yellow spotting; *Betula pendula* exhibiting delicate drooping branches with early golden-flecked leaves.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering

- Permanent civic structures fully preserved: stone kerbs, perimeter cast-iron railings, historical lantern standards, lawn turf, mature trees, and civic monuments.
