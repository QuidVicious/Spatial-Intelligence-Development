# Spatial Domain Analysis: 7 Ainslie Pl, Edinburgh EH3 6AS, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, interbedded with Edinburgh Volcanic Complex basaltic intrusive sills.
Lithics: Primary load-bearing masonry composed of pale grey-blonde Craigleith and Hailes carboniferous quartz-arenite sandstone.
Mortar: Traditional hot-mixed calcitic lime mortar with local river-sand aggregate. Subterranean moisture buffered by glacial till deposits.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Typology: 1820s Georgian neoclassical curved residential circus/terrace (Moray Estate, James Gillespie Graham design).
Massing: Curvilinear 3-storey plus sunken basement and pitched attic dormers. Rusticated ground-floor arcading supporting polished ashlar upper levels with molded string courses and classical entablatures.
Fenestration & Geometry: Regular bays of plumb 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sash-and-case windows. Planar-rectified vertical party walls, continuous stone copings, paired ashlar chimney stacks with clay pots. Neoclassical copper dome profile visible along distant roofline.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Masonry: Fine-grained, droved and polished pale buff Craigleith sandstone ashlar with historic coal-smoke gypsum crusts in sheltered cornices.
Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish metamorphic blue-grey slate with lead flashings and zinc dormer cheeks.
Ironwork: Cast-iron spear-head boundary railings, quadrant balustrades, and boot scrapers coated in black oil-based paint.
Pavement & Turf: Whinstone/granite kerbs, bitumen roadway, compacted gravel paths, and dense perennial grass turf.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Species: Ainslie Place central garden featuring *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
Phenology (Late September): Fully expanded mature broadleaf canopies exhibiting early seasonal chlorosis; localized lime-green to pale ochre edge senescence with partial inner-canopy defoliation.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, traffic infrastructure, road barriers, refuse bins, and pedestrians completely eliminated. Retained civil fixtures: cast-iron boundary railings, dressed stone retaining walls, flagstones, and mature trees.
