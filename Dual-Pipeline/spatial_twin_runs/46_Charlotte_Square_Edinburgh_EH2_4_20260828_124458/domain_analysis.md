# Spatial Domain Analysis: 46 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock & Substratum: Subterranean strata of the Carboniferous Gullane Formation (part of the Strathclyde Group) composed of sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone beds overlain by dense glacial till.
- Masonry Lithics: High-purity silica-rich Craigleith Sandstone ("liver rock", pale honey-to-grey quartzose arenite) utilized for ashlar dressings, portico columns, and palace facades. Secondary structural coursing and basement walls utilize Hailes and Redhall sandstone.
- Memorial Lithic Fabric: Polished pink Peterhead granite pedestal supporting bronze monumental statuary.
- Mortar Chemistry: Traditional non-hydraulic to feebly hydraulic slaked hot-mixed lime mortar with local graded river sand aggregate.
- Groundwater/Drainage: Well-drained raised plateau sloping westward towards the Water of Leith basin; stabilized subterranean water table beneath municipal stormwater infrastructure.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Core Monument (Center Distance): West Register House (formerly St George's Church, completed 1814 by Robert Reid after Robert Adam's 1791 masterplan). Neoclassical monument featuring a giant tetrastyle Ionic portico, ashlar podium, stepped drum with alternating column bays, and a high ribbed hemispherical dome terminating in an octagonal lantern and gilt finial.
- Flanking Terraces (North and South Quadrants): Category A-listed Neoclassical Georgian palace-block facades designed by Robert Adam. 3-storey plus attic and sunken basement configurations, rusticated ground floors with round-arched openings, giant-order pilasters on end pavilions, continuous balustraded parapets, and regular arrays of 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case fenestration.
- Central Garden Monument (Foreground Left): Prince Albert Memorial (unveiled 1876, Sir John Steell) comprising an equestrian bronze statue atop an architectural multi-tiered Peterhead granite pedestal flanked by corner allegorical bronze groups.
- Geometric Rectification: Absolute plumb vertical alignment applied to portico columns, pilasters, townhouse wall planes, and chimney stacks, rectifying photogrammetric triangulation warping into crisp planar ashlar and sharp modillioned cornices.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House: Finely dressed droved and polished Craigleith ashlar sandstone; timber sash fenestration; oxidized copper sheeting with turquoise/verdigris carbonate patina across the dome and lantern.
- Residential Terraces: Droved and polished blonde Craigleith sandstone ashlar facades; cast-iron basement lightwell railings with spearhead finials; dark slate mansard and pitched roofs with lead flashings and dressed sandstone ashlar chimney stacks.
- Pedestrian Surfaces & Basements: Hailes sandstone flagstones and whinstone kerbstones.
- Environmental Weathering & Pathology: Historic bituminous coal-smoke carbon crusting in sheltered ashlar recesses, entablatures, and carved capitals; differential micro-scaling and light gypsum crusting on exposed sandstone cornices; clean rain-washed buff sandstone relief planes.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Botanical Classification: Mature urban garden parkland featuring *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore maple), *Tilia × europaea* (Common lime), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych elm), and *Betula pendula* (Silver birch).
- Phenological Canopy State (August): Dense, fully expanded deep-green summer foliar mass; robust crown coverage with mature leaf retention; fine branch architecture mostly enclosed within the heavy green canopy; manicured perennial turf lawn in foreground.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Total elimination of all motor vehicles, delivery vans, touring coaches, and tarmac parking demarcations.
- Total removal of pedestrians, cyclists, temporary event staging, plastic rubbish bins, construction barriers, and portable commercial signage.
- Retained elements: Permanent Georgian wrought and cast-iron perimeter garden railings, cast-iron street lamps, dressed granite plinths, and permanent stone flagstones.
