# Spatial Domain Analysis: Hobart House, Edinburgh, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
* **Bedrock Stratigraphy**: Lower Carboniferous sedimentary calciferous sandstone measures interspersed with volcanic dolerite intrusions of the Midland Valley basin.
* **Lithic Composition**: Primary construction in fine-to-medium grained Edinburgh New Town sandstones (Craigleith and Hailes pale cream-buff to honey quartz-arenite ashlar); roofing in Scottish Ballachulish and Welsh Bangor grey-black metamorphic slates.
* **Mortar Chemistry**: Slaked hot-lime mortar binders with graded quartz sand aggregates and crushed crushed ceramic/pozzolan additives.
* **Subterranean Dynamics**: Compact glacial till over sedimentary bedrock; subterranean vaults and foundation drainage channels running northward toward the Water of Leith drainage basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
* **Typology & Massing**: 3- to 5-storey urban tenement and commercial block development at the intersection of Hanover Street and Thistle Street. Prominent Scottish Baronial / Renaissance revival corner tenement featuring a polygonal corner turret (tourelle) with a conical slate roof, flanked by classical late 18th-century Georgian and 20th-century contextual New Town terraces.
* **Roof & Vertical Geometry**: Pitched and mansard slate roofs, lead-rolled hips and valleys, ashlar-coped skews, piended and pedimented timber dormers, and droved ashlar chimney stacks with cylindrical terracotta chimney pots.
* **Fenestration**: Rectilinear fenestration grid containing timber sash-and-case windows (predominantly 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 configurations) inset within dressed sandstone reveals and polished architraves.
* **Planar Rectification**: All photogrammetric distortions, bowing walls, and melted roof facets rectified into rigid planar masonry planes, plumb vertical party walls, and razor-straight cornices.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
* **Primary Facades**: Droved cream sandstone ashlar, rusticated ground-floor pilastrades, polished string courses, moulded eaves cornices, and coursed rubble back-elevations.
* **Secondary Elements**: Natural Welsh/Scotch slate tiling, rolled lead flashing, cast-iron rainwater downpipes and hopper heads, wrought-iron ground-level railings.
* **Weathering & Pathology**: Deep atmospheric carbon-soot encrustation in undersides of cornices and window lintels; clean rain-wash strips across projecting stonework; oxidized copper and lead patinas; mild salt efflorescence and ferrous mineral staining along historic sandstone mortar beds.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
* **Species Identified**: Mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore Maple) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime) nestled in rear court perimeters.
* **Phenological State**: Full dense late-summer canopy volume; deep green chlorophyll saturation across broad lobed foliage prior to autumn senescence; dense structural branch architecture obscured by leaf mass.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
* All vehicular traffic, parked cars, delivery vans, pedestrians, wheelie bins, temporary commercial signs, and mobile street clutter completely removed. Fixed civil street furniture (granite/whinstone kerbstones, Caithness flagstone footways, cast-iron streetlamp columns, perimeter area railings) fully retained and cleaned.
