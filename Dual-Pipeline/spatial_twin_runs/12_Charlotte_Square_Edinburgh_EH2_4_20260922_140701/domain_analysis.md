# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Subterranean Bedrock: Dinantian (Lower Carboniferous) Gullane Formation strata comprising sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone beds overlain by Quaternary glaciomarine till deposits.
- Masonry Lithics: Pale cream-to-buff Carboniferous quartzose Craigleith Sandstone, finely dressed in ashlar blocks for structural elevations and classical dressings. Monument base composed of fine-grained pink Peterhead granite ashlar.
- Mortar System: Traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic slaked lime mortar blended with sharp river sand.
- Hydrogeology: Free-draining urban garden topsoil and gravelly subsoil over undulating bedrock sloping north-westward toward the Water of Leith catchment.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Monumental Anchor (West Register House / former St George's Church): Neoclassical temple-front church terminating the George Street axis. Features a giant tetrastyle Roman Ionic portico supporting a dentiled triangular pediment and blocking course. Above sits a square attic pedestal transitioning to a cylindrical drum with attached colonnade, round-arched apertures, an octagonal lead-and-copper hemispherical ribbed dome, and a crowning cupola lantern with gilt finial.
- Flanking Pavilions & Terraces: Symmetrical 3-storey, basement, and attic Georgian residential townhouse ranges (Adam-designed scheme). Rusticated channel-jointed ground floor with round-arched entry doorways; smooth ashlar piano nobile with molded architraves and console cornices; regular 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sliding sash windows; continuous molded eaves cornice and parapet.
- Roof Geometry: Mansard and pitched timber roof trusses clad in graded Scottish slates with pitched dormers, lead flashings, and rectilinear ashlar chimney stacks with ceramic pots.
- Albert Memorial: Multi-stage stepped pink granite pedestal supporting an over-life-sized bronze equestrian statue of Prince Albert in Field Marshal uniform, flanked by lower-tier corner bronze sculptural plinth groupings and bronze bas-relief panels.
- Planar Rectification: True-gravity vertical alignment of columns, portico piers, drum pilasters, lantern uprights, and townhouse facade walls; horizontal rectification of cornices, stringcourses, and sash lines.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Polished and droved Craigleith sandstone ashlar with subtle tooling marks and square-dressed joints.
- Dome & Lantern: Standing-seam copper sheet with mature pale-green verdigris oxide patina, lead flashings, and cast-iron supporting framework.
- Roof Coverings: Dark blue-grey Ballachulish/Scots slates, rolled-sheet lead valleys, and cast-iron downpipes and hoppers.
- Statuary & Plinth: Polished pink Peterhead granite plinth courses with cast patinated dark-brown and oxidized-green statuary bronze.
- Weathering Pathologies: Localized particulate crusting and historic coal-soot deposition in recessed moldings, fluting, and under-eaves cornices; biogenic greening along lower base courses; rain-wash clean lines on projecting Craigleith entablatures.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Urban Flora: Mature specimen broadleaf trees including *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), and *Tilia × europaea* (common lime).
- Phenology (Late September): Early autumn canopy transition; thinning foliage showing chlorotic edge yellowing, pale ochre senescence, and early branch exposure through partial defoliation.
- Turf Cover: Uniform, manicured urban park lawn consisting of *Lolium perenne* (perennial ryegrass) and *Festuca rubra* (red fescue).

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Total exclusion of transient motor vehicles, delivery vans, temporary festival tents, pedestrian traffic, trash receptacles, temporary hoardings, and photogrammetry surface mesh artifacts.
- Retention of fixed civil infrastructure: perimeter sandstone dwarf walls with cast-iron spearhead boundary railings, granite street kerbstones, and the Albert Memorial monument.
