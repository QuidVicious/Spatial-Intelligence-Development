High-angle 35mm documentary film photograph capturing an expansive rural farmstead nestled in Strathspey near Aviemore, looking southwest towards the rolling heather-clad Monadhliath foothills under warm October golden hour sunlight. 

In the middle ground, a cluster of Scottish agricultural steading buildings rests on a gentle plateau: whitewashed lime-harled stone farm cottages with dark slate roofs sit adjacent to wide-span corrugated metal livestock barns and weathered timber-clad sheds. Farm tracks of compacted ochre and pale grey crushed granite gravel curve between the yards and intersect green sheep pasture enclosures bounded by rustic post-and-wire fencing. 

The surrounding landscape transitions into undulating glaciated moorland, dotted with Scots pine (Pinus sylvestris), golden autumn birch copses, and patches of burnt amber bracken. In the far distance, smooth mountain ridges rise under a soft northern sky. 

Low-angled, directional late-afternoon sunlight at 2800K casts long, warm shadows eastward across the fields, catching the metallic sheen of the barn roofs and the crisp white texture of the harled stone walls. Shot on Kodak Portra 400, crisp optical focus across all focal planes, natural film grain, deep tonal dynamic range, atmospheric mountain haze.
