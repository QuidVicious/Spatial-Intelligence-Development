# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock consists of Lower Carboniferous sedimentary sandstone, siltstone, and shale strata of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups. Superstructure masonry is predominantly pale buff to warm blonde Craigleith and Hailes sandstone. Paving and kerb infrastructure comprise Scottish whinstone (quartz-dolerite) and Caithness flagstone laid with lime-pozzolan mortar.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left elevation: Multi-storey Victorian residential terrace with canted bay projections, rusticated basement and ground tiers, polished ashlar upper storeys, and timber sash-and-case fenestration. Mid-ground center: Gothic Revival ecclesiastical structure featuring a rectilinear tower with corner pinnacles, stepped external buttresses, pointed-arch tracery openings, and pitched nave roof. Planar surfaces rectified to true plumb lines, eliminating mesh deformation.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Ashlar masonry with droved and broached tooling, displaying localized carbonaceous atmospheric patina and rain-washed relief. Pitched roofs clad in Ballachulish grey-black slate with lead flashings, stone skews, and coped chimneystacks with terracotta chimney pots. Boundary demarcated by low sandstone dwarf walls topped with cast-iron spear-point railings coated in dark bitumen paint. Surface plane finished in dense asphaltic macadam with whinstone kerbs.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mixed mature deciduous canopy along the right boundary consisting of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), and *Fagus sylvatica* (European Beech). Late October phenology: partial canopy defoliation (approximately 50% leaf drop), senescent foliage displaying ochre, amber, and dull gold chlorosis, exposing complex bare branch architecture and twig ramification.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, parked cars, pedestrians, modern wheelie bins, plastic cones, and temporary traffic signage removed. Retains fixed architectural masonry, cast-iron railings, stone kerbs, and permanent botanical fixtures.
