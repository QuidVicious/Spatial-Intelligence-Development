# Spatial Domain Analysis: 37 Broughton St, Edinburgh EH1 3JU, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation situated on the Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Midland Valley Basin, composed of interbedded Sandstone, Siltstone, and Mudstone of the Gullane Formation overlain by dense glacial till (boulder clay). Primary historic load-bearing lithics derive from local Upper Carboniferous blond/grey sandstone quarries, predominantly fine-to-medium grained quartz arenite from the Craigleith and Hailes quarries. Structural mortar consists of historic non-hydraulic to feebly hydraulic hot-mixed lime and aggregate blends, with localized modern Portland cement repointing across secondary rear elevations. Groundwater hydrology follows the natural northward descending glacial slope toward the Firth of Forth.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Classic Edinburgh New Town eastern periphery vernacular spanning late 18th to mid-19th-century Georgian and early Victorian tenement typologies alongside lower-scale rear mews and light-industrial infill. Broughton Street main elevations exhibit 3-to-4 storey plus basement and mansard attic massing, rectified to true vertical load-bearing planes. Roofscape features complex intersections of double-pitch gables, Scottish slate roofs with zinc/lead ridge capping, pitched timber-framed clerestories, pitched profile metal warehouse roofs, and flat felt/bitumen membranes. Rear elevations present irregular coursed sandstone masonry with projecting stone chimney stacks, clay chimney pots (cans), and regular vertical fenestration bays fitted with traditional timber-framed 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 sash-and-case windows.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades & Gables: Craigleith blond-to-buff ashlar sandstone on primary frontages; coursed and snecked rubble sandstone on rear tenement elevations and boundary walls.
- Weathering & Pathology: Variable surface sulfation crusts, legacy coal-smoke atmospheric soot staining in sheltered masonry recesses, minor biogenic green algal staining along stone copings, and rain-washed clean stone paths across exposed sandstone bands.
- Roofing & Metals: Diminishing-course Scottish and Welsh blue-grey roofing slate, lead flashings and valleys, cast-iron rainwater rones and downpipes, corrugated profiled metal sheeting across lane workshops, and wired plate glass clerestory lights.
- Infill & Extensions: Modern felt flat roofing with gravel ballast, mineral-surfaced bitumen membranes, and zinc parapet cappings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Rear tenement gardens and Broughton Street Lane boundaries support mature urban deciduous flora dominated by *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Betula pendula* (Silver Birch). Late-August phenological expression exhibits dense, fully expanded dark green foliage with early localized chlorotic margins, firm branch architecture, and zero premature defoliation.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient visual clutter, including moving and parked vehicles, pedestrians, waste bins, commercial skips, temporary signage, and photogrammetric geometric noise artifacts, has been completely eliminated. Pure static architectural and infrastructural survey preservation enforced.
