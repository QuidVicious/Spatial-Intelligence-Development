# Spatial Domain Analysis: 6 Shandwick Pl, Edinburgh EH2 4RN, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean formation composed of Lower Carboniferous sedimentary rock of the Gullane and Wardie Mudstone Formations, interspersed with nearby volcanic basaltic intrusions. Structural load rests upon stiff glaciomarine till over sandstone bedrock. Primary building fabric comprises fine-grained Carboniferous quartz-arenite sourced from local historic quarries: buff-coloured Craigleith sandstone alongside laminated pink-grey Hailes sandstone. Bedding and pointing utilize traditional hot-mixed lime mortar composed of slaked high-calcium quicklime and sharp fluvial aggregate.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Victorian Scots Baronial and Italian Renaissance Revival mixed-use tenement blocks alongside early Victorian Georgian infill. Facades range from 4 to 5 storeys plus mansard and dormered attic storeys. Massing features strapwork and crow-stepped gables, corbelled corner turrets, canted oriel bay stacks, and molded continuous stringcourses. Fenestration consists of timber-framed 1-over-1 and 2-over-2 sliding sash-and-case windows set in planar ashlar reveals. In the mid-distance rises the Italian Romanesque campanile of St George's West Church (David Bryce, 1869), defined by a tall square-plan sandstone tower, round-arched louvred belfry arcades, a bracketed cornice, and an octagonal spire.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Droved and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar, channelled rustication at podium levels, carved sandstone corbels, pilasters, and friezes.
- Roofs: Pitched timber roof trusses clad in dark Scottish Ballachulish slate with lead flashing, roll-top terracotta ridge tiles, and heavy stone chimney stacks.
- Spire: Sandstone masonry shaft terminating in an octagonal spire clad in copper sheeting weathered to pale malachite verdigris.
- Ground Surface: Heavy-duty bitumen asphalt road surfacing with flush-embedded grooved steel tram rails, bordered by dressed grey Aberdeen granite kerbs and natural riven Caithness flagstone pedestrian paving.
- Weathering/Patina: Localized atmospheric coal-smoke soiling and black gypsum encrustation within undersides of cornices and carved reliefs; wind-driven rain-wash paths on exposed ashlar surfaces.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Peripheral urban specimens of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime). Canopy state for late September displays early autumn phenology: dense crowns transitioning from deep green to muted ochre and olive-yellow margins, with localized foliage thinning along upper outer branches.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry distortion artifacts, transient pedestrians, moving vehicles, and temporary street clutter are eliminated. Fixed civic fixtures, including steel overhead line poles, cast-iron streetlamps, granite kerbs, and permanent steel tramway tracks, remain fully preserved and plumb.
