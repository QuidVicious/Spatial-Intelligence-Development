Low-angle aerial documentary photograph captured at 381 meters altitude looking southwest across a traditional Scottish Highland steading and farmhouse near Aviemore, Cairngorms National Park, during a crisp October Golden Hour. Unpaved light-tan gravel access tracks wind through undulating fields of rich green and amber autumn pasture.

Low-angled 2950K raking golden sunlight casts long, crisp shadows stretching northeast across the terrain.
