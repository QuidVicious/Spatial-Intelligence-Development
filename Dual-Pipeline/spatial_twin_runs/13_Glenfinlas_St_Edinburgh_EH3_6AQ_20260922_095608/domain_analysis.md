# Spatial Domain Analysis: 13 Glenfinlas St, Edinburgh EH3 6AQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Gullane Formation sandstones and shales). 
Lithics: Primary ashlar of pale buff Craigleith quartzose sandstone; secondary rubble and foundations of Hailes sandstone; street paving in Scottish whinstone (quartz-dolerite) and Caithness flagstone.
Mortar: Historic non-hydraulic slaked lime and sharp river sand. Subsoil: Glacial boulder clay over solid bedrock.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Typology: Neoclassical Georgian residential circus (Moray Estate, James Gillespie Graham, c. 1822).
Storeys & Massing: 3 storeys plus attic dormers over rusticated sunken basement areas. 
Geometry & Fenestration: Strictly plumbed ashlar facades, regular grid of 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows, projecting horizontal stringcourses, continuous dentilled eaves cornices, pitched slate roofs with substantial ashlar chimney stacks and terracotta pots.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Masonry: Droved and polished Craigleith ashlar; rusticated ground storeys.
Roofing & Ironwork: Ballachulish grey-black slate, lead-dressed gutters and valleys; painted cast-iron spear-head railings and balustrades.
Weathering: Coal-smoke sulfation patina in re-entrant reveals, selective rain-wash scouring on exposed ashlar bands, localized biological algae near basement plinths.
Ground: Bituminous asphalt carriageway, dressed whinstone kerb edging, Caithness stone slab pavements.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Species: Central garden canopy dominated by *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime).
Phenology: Late September condition; full canopy transitioning into early autumn chlorosis with yellowing leaf margins and sparse foliage thinning.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicular photogrammetry artifacts (including red mesh noise), pedestrians, dumpsters, and road clutter removed. Fixed historical infrastructure (railings, kerbs, mature flora) rectified and retained.
