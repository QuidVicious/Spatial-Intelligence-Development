# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Ainslie Pl, Edinburgh EH3 6AT, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata (Calciferous Sandstone Measures of the Edinburgh Midland Valley basin).
- Building Lithics: Fine-to-medium grained pale buff Craigleith and Hailes sandstones; Carboniferous quartz-arenite.
- Mortar Chemistry: Traditional hot-mixed lime mortar with local river aggregate and pozzolanic additives.
- Hydrology: Well-drained glacial till over basaltic intrusions, sloping towards the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Typology: Neoclassical Georgian circus townhouses (Moray Estate, 1822–1830, designed by James Gillespie Graham).
- Massing & Storeys: Four storeys including rusticated basement lightwells and attic storeys with slate-hung dormers.
- Planar Rectification: All facade planes rectified plumb vertical; continuous horizontal string courses, plat bands, and cornice balustrades aligned.
- Fenestration: Symmetrical grids of 12-pane and 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows set within droved ashlar reveals.
- Roof Geometry: Timber pitched roofs clad in Scottish slate with regular ashlar chimney stacks and terracotta pots.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Polished and droved Craigleith sandstone ashlar with historic coal-smoke encrustation and rain-wash patinas.
- Basement & Retaining: Raked-joint Craigleith rubble and rusticated sandstone blocks.
- Metalwork: Wrought and cast-iron spearhead perimeter railings and cast-iron decorative balcony guards.
- Ground Surfaces: Mown turf, rolled hoggin/gravel footpaths, whinstone and granite kerbstones, macadamized road surface.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Canopy Taxa: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Fraxinus excelsior* (Ash), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), and *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved Lime).
- Phenology (Late October): Autumnal senescence; canopies display 40–60% defoliation, yellow-brown chlorotic leaves, exposed branching structure, and fallen leaf litter on turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, traffic cones, pedestrians, temporary signage, and refuse bins removed. Static civil infrastructure (cast-iron street lamp standards, perimeter railings, stone kerbing) preserved.
