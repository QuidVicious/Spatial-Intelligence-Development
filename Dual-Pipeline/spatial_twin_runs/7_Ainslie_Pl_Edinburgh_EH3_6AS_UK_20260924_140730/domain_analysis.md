# Spatial Domain Analysis: 7 Ainslie Pl, Edinburgh EH3 6AS, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock sequence of the Inverleith Member (sandstone and siltstone). Superstructural masonry composed of regional Carboniferous Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone. Lime-putty and local sand mortar beds. Well-drained glacial till subsoil over basaltic volcanic bedrock.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
1820s Georgian neoclassical residential crescent circus (Moray Estate plan by James Gillespie Graham). Rectilinear three-storey plus attic and basement massing arranged in curving circus terraces. Ground floors feature channelled ashlar rustication with radiating voussoirs over segmental-arched openings; upper levels display smooth-dressed droved ashlar. Continuous moulded string courses, deep eaves cornices, and plain blocking courses. Pitched roofs clad in Scotch slate punctuated by regular ashlar dormers and substantial chimney stacks with terracotta pots. Rectangular timber sash-and-case fenestration aligned in uniform vertical bays. Distant copper-clad dome visible beyond roofline.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Buff Craigleith sandstone ashlar with subtle carbon soot encrustation, dressed smooth on upper levels, rusticated at base.
- Roofs: Grey-blue Ballachulish slate with lead flashing, ridges, and valleys.
- Joinery: Painted timber multi-pane sash frames (6-over-6 and 12-pane configurations).
- Boundary/Ironwork: Wrought and cast-iron spearhead railings on dressed sandstone plinths.
- Ground Surface: Natural fine gravel and earth pathways bounded by mown turf; perimeter roadway in asphalt and whinstone kerbstones.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Circus park interior anchored by mature *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (common lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (wych elm). Late September phenological state: dense mature crowns beginning early autumnal chlorosis, displaying green foliage intermixed with dull yellowing margins, light leaf loss, and exposed interior limb architecture. Ground cover consists of managed *Poa pratensis* and *Festuca rubra* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
Confirmed. All transient vehicular traffic, pedestrians, waste receptacles, and modern temporary street elements are purged. Fixed architectural boundaries, kerbs, iron railings, and historic stone fabric retained.
