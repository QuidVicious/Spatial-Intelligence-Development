# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary sequences (calciferous sandstone series) overlain by volcanic basalt intrusions.
- Lithic Provenance: Craigleith and Hailes pale blonde-to-buff Carboniferous sandstones, polished Peterhead red granite accents, slate roofing from Ballachulish.
- Mortar & Subterranean Dynamics: Non-hydraulic to feebly hydraulic hot-mixed lime mortars with crushed river quartz aggregate; glacial till drainage beneath urban garden subsoils.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Core Monument: Fluted Roman Doric column (Melville Monument) on a square rusticated pedestal base surmounted by stone heroic statuary.
- Urban Fabric: Georgian Classical New Town terraces (3-to-4 storeys, rusticated ground tiers, ashlar piano nobiles, continuous horizontal string courses) interspersed with late Victorian/Edwardian commercial blocks and late-20th-century framed infill.
- Roofscapes: Pitched and mansard slate roofs, straight-edge lead gutters, dormers, and ashlar chimneystacks.
- Rectification: True-plumb vertical facade lines, rectilinear fenestration grids with 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sash-and-case windows, flat structural floorplates.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Fine-grained Craigleith ashlar, rusticated sandstone basements, dressed stone balustrades, lead flashing, cast-iron perimeter railings.
- Modern Infill: Polished reconstituted stone, precast concrete spandrels, anodized aluminum curtain walling, structural glazing panels.
- Surfaces: Asphalt carriageways, cut granite setts, cast steel tram rails embedded in polymer-sealed concrete slab track, compacted gravel pathways, turf.
- Weathering/Patina: Carbon-crust sooty deposits in sheltered moldings, biological micro-algal growth on shaded cornices, chemical rain-wash staining on exposed sandstone faces.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Dominant Taxa: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved lime), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych elm), *Betula pendula* (Silver birch).
- Phenology (Late September): Fully expanded, mature summer canopies displaying early senescence with localized chlorosis, edge-browning, and minor leaflet drop; mown perennial rye-grass (*Lolium perenne*) lawn.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Static civil fabric only: All vehicles, buses, trams, pedestrians, temporary commercial hoardings, advertising wraps, and transient clutter fully omitted. Retained: structural railings, tram tracks, stone curbs, streetlamps, monument, and fixed garden paths.
