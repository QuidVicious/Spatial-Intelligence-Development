Nadir high-altitude aerial view of an isolated traditional Scottish Highland farm steading near Aviemore, Cairngorms. The central complex consists of a steep gabled corrugated iron agricultural shed with inset roof lights, flanked by auxiliary stone steadings with weathered rust-patinated tin roofs, and a classic whitewashed granite stone residential cottage with slate roof and twin gable-end chimneys. Winding single-track roads of pale crushed granite gravel and compressed earth cut through lush pasture, bracken, and dense heather terrain. Deep green riparian tree canopies along the northern stream boundary. Illumination rendered in late-afternoon October golden hour: low-raking sunlight from the southwest casting elongated, crisp geometric shadows across the undulating grassland; warm 3200K amber highlights contrasting against cool ambient blue skylight in shaded ground crevices; authentic fine film grain, tactile micro-textures of corrugated metal, rough lime harl, and heather moorland, sharp focus, 35mm photograph.
