A crisp eye-level telephoto 35mm documentary photograph capturing the 4-storey, 23.5-meter-tall Washington State Jumbo Mark II class ferry vessel moored into the Bainbridge Island Ferry slip, flanked by the 2-storey, 11.2-meter steel-framed terminal walkway and multi-lane vehicle loading trestle. To the west, the 3-storey, 12-meter Winslow shoreline condominiums step along the rocky littoral edge, while 4-storey, 15.8-meter mixed-use residential complexes rise on the terraced bluff amidst dense mature Douglas fir and Western redcedar forest. Pristine midday Pacific Northwest daylight washes over the deep navy and viridian waters of Eagle Harbor, casting sharp vertical shadows beneath the ferry’s hull, the concrete dock pillars, and the wooden wingwalls. Micro-textures include oxidized marine steel with evergreen and white paint coatings, non-slip textured tarmac in the vehicle holding lanes, wet shingle sand and drift logs along the intertidal shoreline, and reflective double-glazed fenestration on the coastal buildings. Shot on 35mm Ektachrome 100, fine grain, neutral optical clarity, rectilinear geometry perfectly plumbed to true gravity vertical.
