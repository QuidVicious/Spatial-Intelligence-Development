# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock foundation comprises Lower Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Edinburgh Lothian succession, overlain by consolidated glacial till. Primary structural masonry is pale blonde-to-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstone, characterized by high-silica quartzose cementation and fine-to-medium grain. Pedestal of the central equestrian monument constructed from polished red Peterhead granite blocks with grey granite footings. Traditional slaked lime (non-hydraulic) mortar joints throughout historic ashlar masonry.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban ensemble centered on West Register House (formerly St. George's Church, designed by Robert Reid after Robert Adam, 1811–1814). Central edifice features a monumental Roman-Doric tetrastyle entrance portico, entablature, and pediment, surmounted by a stepped square plinth, circular peristyle drum with attached colonnettes, and a tall verdigris-clad copper dome topped by an octagonal lantern and gilded cross. Symmetrical flanking wings comprise Class A-listed Georgian terrace townhouses: three principal storeys over rusticated raised basements, ashlar stringcourses, dentiled eaves cornices, blocking courses, and pitched timber roofs clad in Ballachulish slate punctuated by regular dormers and stone ashlar chimney stacks with terracotta chimney pots. Fenestration is strictly aligned 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 double-hung timber sash-and-case windows recessed in moulded architraves. Foreground features the Prince Albert Memorial (Sir John Steell, inaugurated 1876): multi-tiered stepped red granite pedestal with sculpted bronze corner groups and relief panels supporting a life-size bronze equestrian statue.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- West Register House & Pavilions: Tooled blonde Craigleith sandstone ashlar with fine droved margins, banded channel rustication at ground tier, smooth ashlar above, showing historic carbon-soot patina in sheltered cornices and light rain-washed friezes. Lead flashing and ridge cappings.
- Central Dome: Standing-seam copper sheeting with heavy, uniform pale-green oxidized verdigris patination.
- Memorial: Polished deep-red Peterhead granite ashlar pedestal; cast bronze equestrian statuary with dark bronze weathering and pale green copper carbonate runoff along sculpted drapery and plinth recesses.
- Ground Surfaces & Boundaries: Expansive, mown perennial turfgrass; perimeter boundary marked by cast-iron spearhead railings with urn-capped standards set into droved sandstone dwarf plinth walls.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Winter phenological state for late December. Deciduous urban parkland trees comprising mature *Tilia cordata* (small-leaved lime), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), and *Ulmus glabra* (wych elm). All trees are fully dormant and completely defoliated, displaying bare, complex networks of dark grey-brown ramified branches, knobby trunks, and fine twig silhouettes. Turf is dormant cool-season lawn (*Lolium perenne* blended with *Festuca rubra*), trimmed and lightly moisture-retentive with fine moss infill in shaded areas.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient visual noise suppressed: zero pedestrians, zero motor vehicles along perimeter roadway, zero street waste bins, zero temporary traffic signage, and zero utility cones. Static civil elements retained: wrought and cast-iron railings, sandstone kerbstones, historic stone pedestal monument, mature deciduous trees, and boundary architecture.
