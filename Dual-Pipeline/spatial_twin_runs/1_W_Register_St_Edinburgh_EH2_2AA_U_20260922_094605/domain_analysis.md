# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sandstones and siltstones of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, overlain by glacial till.
- Masonry Lithics: Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey Carboniferous quartz-arenite sandstone ashlar; secondary Clashach and Blaxter sandstone dressings; Caithness flagstone paving; Scottish whinstone (dolerite) kerbstones.
- Mortar & Drainage: Traditional hot-mixed non-hydraulic lime mortar; natural westward sloping topography draining toward the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Melville Monument: 42-meter fluted Roman Doric column on a stepped rusticated square pedestal, surmounted by a standing stone figure.
- Surrounding Urban Blocks: 4-to-6-storey Georgian New Town neoclassical terraces, Victorian commercial palazzi, and late-20th/early-21st-century curtain-wall/stone-clad office blocks.
- Fenestration & Roofs: Regular grids of 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows, large modern glazed apertures, pitched Scottish slate roofs, lead-rolled flat roof decks, and parapet balustrades.
- Rectification: Planar vertical facades rectified to true gravity plumb; orthogonal grid alignments along perimeter thoroughfares.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Historic Envelopes: Tooled, droved, and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar; rusticated ground floors; lead flashing; slate-hung dormers.
- Modern Infill: Anodized aluminum curtain walling, structural double-glazing, polished granite plinths, precast reconstituted stone panels.
- Weathering & Patina: Localized atmospheric coal-smoke crusting on cornices and capitals, biological micro-crust on sheltered ashlar returns, oxidized copper domes with green verdigris.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Species: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), and *Betula pendula* (Silver Birch).
- Phenology (Late September): Fully expanded, mature canopies exhibiting early autumn senescent chlorosis; mottled deep green, dull yellow, and russet foliage with initial canopy thinning.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient road traffic, double-decker buses, trams, pedestrians, construction banners, temporary signage, and street waste receptacles are fully expunged. Fixed civil fabric—tram rails, iron railings, kerbs, lanterns, and circular garden pathways—is preserved.
