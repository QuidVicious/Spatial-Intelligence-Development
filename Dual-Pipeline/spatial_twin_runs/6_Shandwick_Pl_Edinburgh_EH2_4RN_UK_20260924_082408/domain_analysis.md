# Spatial Domain Analysis: 6 Shandwick Pl, Edinburgh EH2 4RN, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Lower Carboniferous sedimentary formations of the Strathclyde Group (sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone) intersected by volcanic dolerite intrusions. Structural masonry is predominantly blonde and grey Carboniferous sandstone sourced from the Craigleith and Hailes quarries, set in historical non-hydraulic and hydraulic lime mortars blended with local river sand. Subterranean drainage follows historical urban culverts towards the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Linear urban corridor lined with 4- to 5-storey Victorian commercial tenements and Renaissance Revival blocks over ground-floor commercial premises. Facades exhibit rigorous structural verticality, pedimented and scrolled dormers, rusticated base courses, string bands, dentilled cornices, and timber-framed sash-and-case fenestration (2-over-2 and 1-over-1 configurations). In the mid-distance termination rises a prominent Italianate-Romanesque church campanile featuring multi-tiered arched belfry openings, clock dials, and a square-plan copper-clad pyramidal spire.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Tooled, droved, and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar with local carbonaceous soot encrustations in protected cornices, offset by rain-washed lighter upper friezes.
- Roofs: Ballachulish grey-black slate pitched mansards with lead roll ridges, flashing, and zinc valley gutters.
- Campanile Spire: Oxidized verdigris sheet copper with pale green carbonate patina.
- Ground Surface: Bituminous asphalt carriageway inlaid with grooved steel tramway rails, delineated by dressed Scottish whinstone and silver-grey granite kerbs, fronted by Caithness flagstone footways.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Dense urban hardscape environment. Incidental distant ornamental canopy (Tilia cordata / Acer pseudoplatanus) rendered in early-autumn phenology (late September), displaying incipient leaf senescence, localized chlorophyll breakdown along leaf margins, and mottled yellow-green foliage.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry geometric tearing, mesh artifacts, ghosting, transient pedestrians, moving vehicles, and temporary street furniture are completely purged. Fixed civic infrastructure (tram tracks, overhead catenary support poles, and cast-iron streetlamp columns) remains structurally rectified and plumb.
