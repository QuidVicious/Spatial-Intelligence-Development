# Spatial Domain Analysis: 20816 President Point Rd NE, Kingston, WA 98346, USA

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation situated within the Puget Lowland glacial drift sequence, dominated by dense Vashon lodgement till, advance outwash sand and gravel, and glaciolacustrine silt strata. Shoreline margin consists of mixed quaternary beach gravels, sub-rounded basaltic cobbles, and unsorted littoral sand deposits. Drainage is defined by high-permeability surficial slopes discharging toward the marine foreshore.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Pacific Northwest coastal vernacular residential cluster anchored into the hillside contour. Primary dwelling is a two-storey multi-planar timber-frame structure featuring an intersecting gable-and-hipped composite roof system, extensive cantilevered wraparound timber observation decks, and wide multi-lite panoramic glazing facing the waterline. Flanking auxiliary structure comprises a two-storey gabled carriage house typology with ground-level vehicular bays and upper residential volume. Outlying hillside volumes feature stepped single- and multi-level rectilinear footprints. All vertical wall planes, deck posts, and fascia lines rectified to plumb and orthogonal alignments.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Structures clad in horizontal cedar lap weatherboarding and painted fiber-cement siding with crisp corner trim boards. Decks constructed with dimensional pressure-treated structural framing and composite deck planks secured by powder-coated metal and timber railings. Roofs surfaced in architectural dimensional asphalt shingles with continuous ridge vents and metal drip edges. Foundation elements comprise cast-in-place reinforced concrete stem walls anchored directly to glacial till. Salt-fog oxidation on metal fasteners and subtle marine atmospheric biological patina on shaded timber cladding. Ground surface transitions from crushed basalt gravel access lanes to packed earth and native coastal pebble-silt mix.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Dense Pacific Northwest coastal temperate maritime canopy. Dominant evergreen conifers include mature *Pseudotsuga menziesii* (Douglas-fir) and *Thuja plicata* (Western redcedar) with pyramidal crowns and pendulous foliage sprays, interspersed with *Tsuga heterophylla* (Western hemlock). Deciduous understory and forest fringe include *Acer macrophyllum* (Bigleaf maple) displaying early September foliar margin chlorosis, *Alnus rubra* (Red alder), *Gaultheria shallon* (Salal), and ground cover of *Polystichum munitum* (Western sword fern).

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, parked cars, loose shoreline equipment, temporary bins, and pedestrian figures are fully excised. Structural envelopes, fixed timber balustrades, retaining walls, gravel driveways, and native botanical masses remain static and pristine.
