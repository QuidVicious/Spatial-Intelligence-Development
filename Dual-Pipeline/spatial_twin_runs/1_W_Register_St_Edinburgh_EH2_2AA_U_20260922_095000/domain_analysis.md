# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, featuring Calciferous Sandstone measures overlain by glacial till. Primary architectural lithics: pale carboniferous Craigleith and Hailes sandstones (fine-to-medium quartz arenite), with Cullalo and Clashach sandstone in restorative phases. Mortar: historic non-hydraulic lime-sand mixes with carbonaceous particulate inclusions.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian New Town urban plan centered on an elliptical garden enclosure. Centerpiece: Melville Monument (42-metre fluted Roman Doric column atop a rusticated square plinth). Surrounding perimeter: 4-to-6-storey Georgian Classical and Victorian Palazzo-style ashlar commercial frontages, punctuated by late-20th-century concrete frame and modern glass-and-steel commercial infill. Roofscapes feature timber rafter profiles, Ballachulish slate pitches, lead-dressed dormers, and ashlar chimney stacks. Fenestration: 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sash-and-case grids alongside modern planar curtain-wall glazing.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Polished and droved Craigleith sandstone ashlar; rusticated ground-floor coursing; historic atmospheric coal-smoke gypsum crusts and rain-washed relief surfaces.
- Modern Infill: Anodized aluminum framing, precast reconstituted stone panels, and low-iron structural glazing.
- Roofing & Accents: Scottish Ballachulish slate, rolled lead valleys, oxidized verdigris copper dome copings, and cast-iron balustrades.
- Ground Plane: Caithness flagstone paving, granite kerb stones, asphalt roadways, and tramway steel rails embedded in polymeric-grouted concrete trackbeds.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban civic parkland containing *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), *Tilia cordata* (small-leaved lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (wych elm). Late September phenology: dense canopies exhibiting early foliar chlorosis, patchy yellowing, and minor outer-canopy defoliation. Interior central lawn composed of perennial *Lolium perenne* and *Poa pratensis* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicular traffic (buses, cars, trams), temporary scaffolding wraps, pedestrians, refuse bins, and temporary street furniture are excised. Permanent street infrastructure, iron railings, stone bollards, tracks, and mature vegetation remain fully intact.
