# Spatial Domain Analysis: 46 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- Bedrock: Carboniferous sedimentary sandstone, siltstone, and mudstone of the Strathclyde Group beneath glacial till.
- Masonry Lithics: Craigleith and Hailes sandstone (pale buff to grey quartz-arenite).
- Mortar: Historic hydraulic lime with sharp local aggregate.
- Subterranean: Glacial till over volcanic/sandstone basement with urban storm drainage.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- Centerpiece: West Register House (former St. George's Church), Robert Reid/Robert Adam design; colossal tetrastyle portico, square clock tower base, peristyle drum with Corinthian columns, hemispherical copper dome, lantern cupola.
- Flanking Terraces: Neo-Classical Georgian residential palatial frontages; 3 storeys over rusticated basements, ashlar facades, timber 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 sash-and-case windows, projecting pedimented pavilions, slate mansard roofs with dressed stone chimney stacks and dormers.
- Monument: Prince Albert Memorial equestrian bronze atop a stepped pink granite and sandstone pedestal.
- Planar Rectification: Rectified vertical load-bearing lines, planar ashlar facades, aligned multi-tier window grid, corrected dome curvature.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith buff-grey fine-grained sandstone ashlar, V-grooved channelled rustication at ground tier, smooth ashlar upper tiers.
- Dome: Copper sheathing weathered to verdigris patina, sandstone drum and balustrades.
- Roofing: Scottish and Welsh slate tiles, lead flashing, stone copings.
- Weathering: Coal-smoke atmospheric sulfate crusts on sheltered cornices, biological patination, rain-washed clean ashlar elevations.
- Ground Surface: Maintained rye-fescue lawn, sandstone boundary kerbstones, asphalt perimeter roadway.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- Deciduous Canopy: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
- Phenology (December 22): Full winter dormancy; bare branch silhouettes, void of foliage, exposed ramified crown architecture over dormant winter turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Complete exclusion of all transient motor vehicles, tour buses, temporary signage, roadworks, and pedestrians. Static civil infrastructure (cast-iron railings, stone curbs, fixed lampposts) fully preserved.
