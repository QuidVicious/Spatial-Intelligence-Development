High-resolution, award-winning 35mm documentary photograph looking slightly downward from an aerial elevation of 352.6 meters facing north across the Spey Valley near Aviemore. A prominent 2-storey harled fieldstone croft farmhouse rising to 7.8 meters in ridge height forms the core of the cluster, flanked by an elongated 1-storey stone byre of 4.5 meters and a detached 5.2-meter single-storey agricultural barn with aged corrugated iron roofing. The cluster of Highland farm buildings sits nestled in vast, rolling pastoral green fields framed in the background by the smooth, undulating crest of heather-covered moorland hills beneath a clear, expansive northern sky. A light-toned unpaved track of packed earth and pale crushed granite curves up toward the farmyard entrance. Rendered during late golden hour under warm 3300K raking sunlight from the west-northwest, casting elongated, razor-sharp shadows across the pasture and picking out the rough relief of lime-harled stone walls and dark slate roofs. Authentic film grain, natural optical depth of field, tactile textures of moorland heather, rough pasture grass, and weathered Highland stone architecture.
