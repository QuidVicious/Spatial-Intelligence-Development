# Spatial Domain Analysis: 18A Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DF, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock & Subsurface:** Glacially smoothed Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock sequence (calciferous sandstone series) overlain by stiff glacial till.
- **Lithic Sourcing:** Fine-grained quartzose Craigleith pale buff-grey sandstone for monumental ashlar facades; carbonaceous Hailes sandstone for foundation courses.
- **Mortar Chemistry:** Non-hydraulic slaked lime binder mixed with coarse quartz river sand.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Central Monument (West Register House):** Neoclassical monumental church design (Robert Reid/Robert Adam, 1814). Features a rusticated basement, colossal Ionic tetrastyle pedimented portico, stepped square attic, circular peristyle drum with Corinthian columns, and a hemispherical copper-clad dome capped by an open stone lantern.
- **Residential Pavilions (Charlotte Square Façades):** Symmetrical 3-storey plus attic and basement Georgian townhouse blocks. Features channelled ashlar rustication on ground floors, upper-storey ashlar masonry, crisp molded string courses, dentiled cornices, 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 double-hung timber sash-and-case windows, ashlar chimney stacks, and Ballachulish slate roofs.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Masonry & Cladding:** Pale buff Craigleith sandstone ashlar with broached and droved tool finishes; verdigris-patinated sheet copper dome; Ballachulish grey-black roofing slates; lead roll flashings.
- **Ground & Boundary Elements:** Dressed whinstone kerbs; Caithness stone flagstone sidewalks; asphalt road carriage surface; black painted wrought-iron spear-headed perimeter railings.
- **Weathering & Pathology:** Localized urban soot crusting in sheltered cornices; historic sulfate crusting; rain-washed clean ashlar projections.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Species Composition:** Mature urban specimens of *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved lime), and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore).
- **Date Phenology (Late September):** Full-volume canopies exhibiting early autumn chlorosis, foliage turning pale lime-green to ochre with modest leaf loss exposing structural branch framework.
- **Ground Flora:** Maintained *Festuca rubra* and *Poa pratensis* parkland turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, pedestrians, waste bins, temporary barriers, and modern traffic clutter are fully expunged. Static historic infrastructure (wrought-iron railings, stone gateposts, perimeter kerbs) remains intact.
