# Spatial Domain Analysis: Playfair House, Broughton St Ln, Edinburgh EH1 3LY, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock Lithology:** Carboniferous sedimentary sequences of the Gullane Formation (Strathclyde Group), characterized by interbedded sandstones, siltstones, and mudstones situated over the Calton volcanic bedrock periphery.
- **Building Lithics:** Primary construction utilizing blonde-to-grey Craigleith quartz-arenite sandstone, Hailes micaceous laminated sandstone for coursed rubble rear elevations, and fine-grained Darney/Cullalo sandstone dressings.
- **Mortar Chemistry:** Traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic slaked lime mortar blended with sharp river aggregate; localized 20th-century Portland cement repointing at low courses.
- **Hydrology & Drainage:** Glacial till over permeable sedimentary strata; regional subterranean gradient draining northeast towards the Water of Leith basin.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Typology:** Late Georgian New Town multi-storey tenement blocks (circa 1800–1820) enclosing mews back-lanes, juxtaposed with 20th-century rear infill structures (Playfair House complex).
- **Storey Massing & Envelope:** 4-to-5 storey primary tenement elevations with attic storeys, stepped down to 1-to-3 storey pitched-roof mews wings and flat-roofed annexes.
- **Fenestration:** Regular vertical fenestration grid of timber sash-and-case windows (12-pane, 6-over-6, and Victorian 2-over-2 configurations) set in recessed stone margins with projected sills and flat arched lintels.
- **Roof Geometry:** Pitched dual-pitch roofs clad in diminishing-course Scottish and Welsh blue-grey slates, punctuated by gabled and piended slate-cheeked dormers, stone coped skews, lead valleys, and multi-flue sandstone chimney stacks with terracotta pots.
- **Planar Rectification:** Strict vertical alignment of tenement load-bearing party walls, planarization of rear elevations, plumb chimney stacks, and sharp rectification of eaves lines.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Historic Masonry:** Random and snecked squared sandstone rubble to secondary rear elevations; droved ashlar sandstone rybats, cills, lintels, and quoins.
- **Modern Interventions:** Mineral-felt flat roof membranes, exposed precast concrete beam lintels, steel access balustrades, and painted render infill panels.
- **Pathology & Weathering:** Industrial coal-smoke carbon crusts on protected sandstone surfaces, granular disaggregation and exfoliation from historic atmospheric sulfur deposition, iron-oxide staining near downpipes, and localized green algal biofilm along shaded base plinths.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Botanical Identifiers:** Courtyard specimens of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore maple) and *Fraxinus excelsior* (European ash), with boundary colonization by *Hedera helix* (English ivy).
- **Phenology (Late Summer / August):** Heavy, fully expanded deep-green foliage; mature broad canopy volume with localized leaf-margin desiccation; dense vegetative undergrowth along courtyard garden plots.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Total elimination of all vehicles, parked cars, delivery vans, wheelie bins, dumpsters, commercial skips, temporary signage, and pedestrians.
- Preservation of static civil infrastructure: Whinstone/granite lane setts, sandstone boundary walls, cast-iron rainwater downpipes, and iron boundary railings.
