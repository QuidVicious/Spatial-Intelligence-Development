# Spatial Domain Analysis: 46 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4HQ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean strata comprise Lower Carboniferous sandstones and siltstones of the Inverclyde Group, overlain by dense glacial till. Primary structural lithics consist of pale buff-grey quartz-arenite Craigleith sandstone ashlar, backed with Hailes sandstone rubble masonry. Bedding planes laid on natural quarry beds with non-hydraulic lime mortar pointing.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical unified civic ensemble. Centerpiece: West Register House (former St George’s Church), featuring an advanced tetrastyle Ionic portico, stepped rusticated base, and a high circular peristyle drum supporting a ribbed copper dome and lantern. Flanked symmetrically by continuous 3-storey plus basement and attic Georgian residential terraces. Ground storeys feature channeled rustication; upper floors display polished ashlar with alternating pedimented and plain 6-over-6 timber sash-and-case windows. Foreground features the central Prince Albert Memorial equestrian bronze atop an elevated multi-tiered ashlar pedestal within an enclosed circular lawn.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Craigleith sandstone ashlar with fine droved and polished finishes, oxidized verdigris sheet copper on the central dome, dark grey Scottish slate roofing, lead flashings, and cast iron area railings. Weathering includes localized coal-smoke gypsum crusts under cornices and rain-washed pale sandstone facades. Mown turf lawn surrounds a bronze-patinated sculptural group on a weathered stone plinth.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban parkland specimens: *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), and *Tilia × europaea* (Common lime). State for late December: fully defoliated winter dormancy with bare branch ramification and exposed trunk bark.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All perimeter vehicles, traffic infrastructure, pedestrian figures, and transient clutter are fully expunged. Fixed historical iron railings, stone kerbs, and monument plinths are retained intact.
