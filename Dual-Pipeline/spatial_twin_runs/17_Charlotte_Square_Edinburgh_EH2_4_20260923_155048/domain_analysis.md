# Spatial Domain Analysis: 17 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4DJ, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation: Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock of the Midland Valley basin. Structural masonry: Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone. Bedding mortar: Traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic slaked lime with coarse local aggregate.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Symmetrical Neo-Classical civic square (designed Robert Adam, executed Robert Reid). Central focal structure (West Register House, formerly St George's Church): Tetristyle Ionic/Corinthian colossal portico with dentilled triangular pediment, rusticated ashlar basement, stepped drum, and ribbed copper dome crowned by an octagonal stone lantern. Flanking terraces: 3-storey plus basement and attic Georgian residential palace-front facades, rusticated ground floors, multi-pane timber sash-and-case fenestration (6-over-6 and 12-over-12), continuous string courses, balustraded parapets, and pitched Ballachulish slate roofs with masonry chimneystacks. Foreground: Central equestrian monument on stepped ashlar pedestal.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Polished and droved Craigleith sandstone ashlar; oxidized verdigris green copper dome sheeting; patinated dark bronze equestrian statuary set on pink Peterhead granite and sandstone plinth; dark grey Scottish roofing slate; wrought and cast iron boundary railings and lamp standards; lead rainwater goods. Subtle atmospheric coal-smoke crusting across recessed moldings and sheltered friezes.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Parkland garden enclosure comprising dormant perennial turf (*Lolium perenne*, *Festuca rubra*). Urban canopy: Mature *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane) and *Tilia cordata* (small-leaved lime). Phenological state for late December: Complete winter defoliation, exposed branching networks, dormant terminal buds, zero foliage.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, pedestrians, dumpsters, temporary barriers, and street clutter removed. Fixed structural civil elements (railings, plinths, static masonry) fully retained.
