# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation composed of Lower Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock belonging to the Strathclyde Group, comprising interbedded blonde sandstones, carbonaceous siltstones, and mudstones overlain by dense glacial till. Primary architectural masonry sourced from local Craigleith and Hailes quarries: fine-to-medium-grained quartz-arenite sandstone with high silica content and carbonaceous laminations. Pedestal foundation blocks utilize polished Peterhead pink granite atop dressed Craigleith bedrock pads. Mortar matrices consist of traditional non-hydraulic lime binders blended with sharp, coarse quartz sands, showing localized modern repointing with natural hydraulic lime (NHL 2.0). Groundwater dynamics governed by shallow seasonal perched water tables beneath parkland topsoil.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban ensemble comprising Robert Adam's uniform residential palace-front terraces (1791–1820) and Robert Reid’s central West Register House (formerly St George's Church, 1811–1814). West Register House features a monumental centralized copper-clad dome on an arcaded drum with Corinthian pilasters, surmounting a rusticated ashlar base with an advanced tetrastyle Ionic portico and flanking pavilion wings. Flanking terraced townhouses adhere to strict symmetrical Georgian orders: rusticated ground-floor ashlar over sunken basements, smooth-dressed piano nobile with pedimented and molded architraves, second-storey square windows, and continuous balustraded parapets. Roofscapes feature pitched slate-clad timber rafters with lead-dressed dormers, chimney stacks with ceramic pots, and ashlar skew copings. Central park features the Prince Albert Memorial (1876, Sir John Steell): bronze equestrian monument atop an ornate stepped granite pedestal with secondary bronze allegorical relief groups at the corners.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith sandstone ashlar with channelled rustication at ground level, fine droved/ashlar dressing above; localized industrial coal-smoke soot staining and rain-wash striping on leeward surfaces.
- Dome: Standing-seam copper sheet cladding exhibiting complete oxidized copper carbonate verdigris (matte pale green).
- Monument: Cast bronze statuary with natural dark olive to verdigris patina, set upon pink Peterhead granite plinths with honed and rusticated surfaces.
- Roofs: Ballachulish Scottish black-grey slates in diminishing courses, lead flashings, ridges, and valley gutters.
- Fenestration: Timber-framed sash-and-case windows (typically six-over-six and twelve-pane configurations) painted off-white.
- Boundaries: Wrought and cast-iron spearhead boundary railings and gates coated in satin black bitumen paint, anchored into dressed sandstone dwarf plinths.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Enclosed urban park lawn (*Lolium perenne*, *Festuca rubra*) showing winter dormancy. Deciduous canopy consisting of mature *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), and *Platanus × acerifolia* (London Plane). Phenology precisely aligned with late December (2026-12-22): 100% defoliation; bare, highly articulated branch architecture; dormant winter buds; dark, wet-barked trunks with localized epiphytic crustose lichens (*Lecanora*) and moss cushions (*Hypnum cupressiforme*) on prevailing weather flanks.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All peripheral traffic, parked vehicles, asphalt road clutter, temporary signage, tourist apparatus, and pedestrian presence fully expunged. Cast-iron lampposts, granite kerbstones, sandstone dwarf walls, perimeter railings, and permanent municipal features preserved in true geometric alignment.
