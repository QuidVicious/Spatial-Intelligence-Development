# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock comprises Carboniferous Lower Oil-Shale sedimentary strata (sandstones, shales) overlain by glacial till. Structural masonry utilizes local pale-ochre to cream-grey Craigleith and Hailes quartz-arenite carboniferous sandstones, bonded with historical non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic lime mortar (slaked quicklime and local sharp river sand aggregate). Subterranean drainage flows down towards the nearby Water of Leith valley.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left foreground/midground: Victorian three-to-four-storey residential tenement terrace with canted ashlar bay windows, moulded stringcourses, cornice lines, and timber double-hung sash-and-case fenestration. Center midground: Gothic Revival ecclesiastical structure featuring a tall battlemented square bell tower with corner crocketed pinnacles, lancet window apertures, buttressed nave, and pitched slate roof. Right side: Curving street alignment bounded by low ashlar coped retaining walls fronting deep garden tree lines. Planar vertical lines rectified to plumb geometry; mesh deformation flattened to true elevations.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Victorian Residential Facades: Fine-dressed ashlar Craigleith sandstone, droved margins, rusticated basement coursing, slate roofing tiles, painted timber window frames. Atmospheric sooty coal-smoke patination on sheltered friezes.
- Church Fabric: Squared coursed rubble and tooled ashlar sandstone dressings; weathered grey-black surface encrustations, oxidized iron fixings, lead valley flashings.
- Enclosure & Street: Wrought/cast-iron spearhead railings seated on dressed sandstone dwarf walls. Tar-bound macadam roadway bordered by squared whinstone setts and Caithness flagstone pedestrian sidewalks.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Belgrave Crescent Gardens vegetation belt (late September phenology): Mature canopy dominated by *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), and *Fraxinus excelsior* (European Ash). Foliage displays early autumn senescence with patchy chlorosis, dull ochre edge-browning, and slight crown thinning. Dense understorey shrubs with dark green fading foliage.
