Cinematic architectural documentary photograph, low-altitude aerial view looking south-southwest across an upland Highland farmstead near Aviemore, Cairngorms National Park. Early morning sunrise in August, low golden sunlight raking from the eastern horizon at an altitude of 12°, casting long crisp shadows westward across dew-covered pastures and heather moorland.

In the foreground, a whitewashed traditional stone cottage and steading with sharp Ballachulish slate roofs, crisp pitched dormer gables, and dark painted window trim. Beyond it sits a large utilitarian agricultural barn complex with clean corrugated roofing panels inset with translucent skylights, flanked by weathered timber and steel outbuildings. Sinuous pale gravel access tracks curve between the structures across rolling glacial till terrain covered in green grasses, patches of blooming purple calluna heather, and scattered Scots pine and silver birch thickets on the rising slopes behind.

Atmosphere features crisp, crystalline morning highland air with light wisps of low-lying ground mist settling in hollows.
