# Spatial Domain Analysis: 15B London St, Edinburgh EH3 6LY, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Subterranean Bedrock**: Carboniferous sedimentary sequence of the Gullane Formation (Strathclyde Group), characterized by interbedded sandstones, siltstones, and bituminous mudstones, overlain by dense Pleistocene glacial till (stiff clay and gravel boulder clay).
- **Lithics & Quarry Sources**: Superstructure constructed from Carboniferous *Craigleith sandstone* (fine-grained quartz arenite "liver rock" ashlar for street-facing facades) and *Hailes sandstone* for foundations and rear walls. Pavements and roadways surfaced in Carboniferous olivine-basalt/whinstone setts and Scottish granite kerbstones. Roofs clad in Scottish *Ballachulish* and North Welsh metamorphic roofing slates.
- **Mortar Chemistry**: Pure slaked non-hydraulic and feebly hydraulic lime (*calcium hydroxide*) mortar mixed with sharp sub-angular pit sand, finished with flush and ruled pencil pointing.
- **Subterranean Drainage**: Gravity-fed groundwater draining northward toward the Water of Leith catchment; stone-vaulted cellar lightwells beneath street-level flagstone platts.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Typology**: Category A listed Georgian New Town terraced tenements and townhouses (designed c. 1820–1823 by Robert Reid and William Sibbald).
- **Storeys & Massing**: 3 storeys plus raised basement lightwells and attic mansards/dormers organized into a unified neoclassical terrace.
- **Planar Rectification & Geometry**: True vertical load-bearing lines, planar ashlar facades, continuous molded eaves cornices, blocking courses, continuous string courses at first-floor level, and planar pitched slate roof lines.
- **Fenestration**: Regular rhythmic grid of recessed timber double-hung sash-and-case windows (12-pane 6-over-6 configurations on upper levels, timber astragals, painted white/off-white), framed by dressed stone architraves with planar splayed reveals.
- **Street-Level Articulation**: Droved and rusticated stone basement area walls, cantilevered stone access bridge platts over excavated lightwells, vertical cast-iron spearhead railings, and substantial stone chimney stacks with terracotta cylindrical chimney pots along party walls.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Primary Facades**: Fine-grained, pale buff to silver-grey Craigleith sandstone ashlar with finely broached and droved margins.
- **Basements & Rear Masonry**: Coursed squared Hailes rubble with raised mortar joints.
- **Metalwork & Joinery**: Cast iron boundary railings with urn and spear finials, wrought iron lamp brackets, dark-painted solid timber four-panel entrance doors with fanlights.
- **Weathering & Pathology**: Historic coal-smoke carbonaceous crusts preserved in sheltered under-cornice friezes and sill recesses, vertical mineral rain-wash tracks, localized iron-oxidation staining, and biological micro-algal greening across the lower basement ashlar plinths.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Terminal Vista Botanical Matrix**: Dense parkland canopy closing the western axial vista comprising *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime).
- **Phenology**: Late-summer mature peak canopy state; heavy foliage density with deep green to olive-drab leaf clusters, firm crown massing, and early micro-chlorosis on outermost exposed leaf margins.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Total elimination of all transient road traffic, parked passenger vehicles, delivery vans, pedestrians, wheelie bins, plastic street clutter, and temporary municipal signage.
- Full retention and restoration of permanent static civil elements: intact whinstone street setts, dressed granite kerbs, flagstone footpaths, basement lightwells, iron railings, and mature trees.
