# Spatial Domain Analysis: 30 N W Circus Pl, Edinburgh EH3 6TP, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean foundation composed of Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock belonging to the Strathclyde Group, overlain by dense glacial lodgement till and boulder clay descending toward the Water of Leith basin. Masonry lithics consist primarily of pale cream-to-buff Craigleith and grey Hailes quartz-rich sandstone, characterized by high silica content, cross-bedding, and exceptional weathering resistance. Bedding joints set in traditional hydraulic lime and sharp river sand mortars. Roadway sub-base capped by olivine-dolerite (whinstone) setts and dressed Scottish granite kerbstones.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian Neoclassical multi-storey residential and commercial tenement terraces descending along a continuous gradient. Four-storey over basement ashlar facades with planarized vertical load-bearing lines. Ground floors exhibit channelled ashlar rustication and integrated 19th-century timber shopfront fascias; upper storeys display polished ashlar with crisp broached margins. Fenestration arranged in uniform vertical grids of timber-framed 12-pane and 6-over-6 sash-and-case windows with deep stone reveals. Rooflines defined by continuous moulded eaves cornices, blocking courses, pitched Scotch slate timber-framed roofs, pitched dormer projections, and substantial ashlar chimney stacks with clay chimney pots. Drop-light basement areas bounded by vertical wrought-iron spearhead railings.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Polished and droved Craigleith blonde sandstone ashlar, exhibiting historical coal-smoke sooty crusting, selective rain-washed pale reveals, and atmospheric sulphate patination on undercut cornices.
- Roofs: Pitched Scots and Welsh blue-grey roofing slates secured with copper nails, dressed lead ridge rolls, valleys, and chimney flashing.
- Fenestration & Joinery: Painted soft-wood sash-and-case frames, painted timber shopfront surrounds with glazed transoms.
- Ironwork: Cast and wrought iron railings, balconies, and rainwater hoppers finished in bitumen black paint with localized ferric oxide weathering.
- Ground Plane: Dressed rectangular whinstone and granite carriageway setts with bound sand-lime pointing, abutting broad tooled sandstone slab pavements and granite curb edging.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Urban dendrological specimens consisting of *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm). Date-grounded December 25 winter state: complete deciduous dormancy, total defoliation, exposing complex architecture of bare structural limbs, rugged grey-brown bark, and intricate branch tracery.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient road traffic, parked motor vehicles, mobile waste bins, plastic cones, modern street signage, and pedestrian figures are fully eliminated. Fixed historical cast-iron lampposts, pavement kerbstones, and basement lightwell railings are preserved.
