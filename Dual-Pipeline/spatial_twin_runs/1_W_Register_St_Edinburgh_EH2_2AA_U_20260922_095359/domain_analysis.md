# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock: Lower Carboniferous sedimentary sandstone (Inverclyde Group / Calciferous Sandstone Measures) overlain by dense glacial till. Primary structural lithics comprise Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone ashlar, bonded with hydraulic lime mortar. Base courses incorporate fine-grained Scottish granite and basalt (whinstone).

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical, Georgian New Town, and Victorian commercial civic architecture anchored by the 46-metre fluted Craigleith sandstone Roman Doric Melville Monument. Surrounding blocks range from 4 to 6 storeys with rusticated basements, piano nobile sash fenestration, dentiled cornices, and ashlar parapets. Infill includes mid-to-late 20th-century reinforced concrete frames with planar curtain-wall glazing. Roofs feature multi-pitched Scottish slate, lead flashing, dormers, and standing-seam copper mansards.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Primary Facades: Craigleith sandstone ashlar with historic coal-smoke carbon crusts and localized rain-wash leaching.
- Secondary Facades: Modern precast concrete panels, anodized aluminium mullions, bush-hammered granite.
- Roofscapes: Blue-grey Scottish Ballachulish slate, rolled lead gutters, oxidized copper sheet with verdigris patina.
- Ground Surfaces: Fine-grained asphalt, polished whinstone setts, Caithness/Yorkstone paving slabs, gravel garden pathways.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban broadleaf canopy within St Andrew Square Gardens. Species include *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), and *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved lime). Late-September phenology: dense canopies exhibiting early chlorosis, yellow-brown margin discoloration, and light leaf shed over manicured *Lolium perenne* turf.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, buses, trams, pedestrians, construction hoardings, dumpsters, and temporary street furnishings have been fully removed. Permanent tram rails, stone kerbs, and iron railings remain intact.
