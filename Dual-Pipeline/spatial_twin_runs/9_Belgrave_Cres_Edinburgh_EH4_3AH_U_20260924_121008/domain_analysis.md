# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary strata of the Edinburgh Midland Valley. Superstructure built from regional calciferous sandstone (primarily Craigleith and Hailes quarry beds). Slaked hot-lime mortar joints. Free-draining sloping ground toward the Water of Leith gorge.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left: Multi-storey late-Victorian residential terrace (c. 1870s) featuring canted bay projections, dressed ashlar margins, moulded stringcourses, and timber sash-and-case fenestration. Straight vertical load-bearing lines with planar facades. Center distance: Neo-Gothic ecclesiastical structure featuring a rectilinear masonry tower, crocketed corner pinnacles, belfry louvres, and pointed-arch lancet windows. Boundary: Low-profile coursed-rubble retaining wall with weathered coping stones.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Fine-grained buff-grey Craigleith sandstone in coursed ashlar and rock-faced rustication. Atmospheric coal-smoke patina and sulphate encrustations on sheltered cornices. Scotch slate pitched roofs with lead roll-top ridging. Cast-iron downpipes, hopper heads, and perimeter railings. Ground surface composed of asphalt roadway bordered by dressed whinstone and granite kerbstones.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Bordering mature urban woodland strip comprising *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore) and *Tilia x europaea* (common lime). Dense branching habit with full crown volume, deep green broadleaf foliage, and organic understorey shrub layer anchoring the soil bank.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh artifacts, distorted parked vehicles, temporary barriers, traffic cones, and pedestrians are removed. Ground plane and roadway are rendered as clean, continuous paved infrastructure.
