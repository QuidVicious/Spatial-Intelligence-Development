# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock; Edinburgh Basin sandstone sequence. Superstructure constructed of local Craigleith and Hailes pale buff-to-grey quartzose sandstone ashlar. Lime-based hydraulic mortar beds; damp, temperate groundwater regime.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Left foreground: High Victorian residential terrace; rectified vertical planar ashlar facades, three-storey elevation with canted multi-tier bay projections, molded string courses, and 2-over-2 / 1-over-1 timber sash-and-case fenestration. Mid-ground center: Neo-Gothic ecclesiastical structure featuring a square bell tower with corner pinnacles, parapet crenellations, buttressed bays, and lancet openings. Low stone boundary retaining wall flanking street edge.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
Craigleith fine-grained sandstone ashlar with stugged and polished dressings; atmospheric coal-smoke crusting and localized rain-wash leaching. Scottish slate pitched roofing with lead flashing and zinc ridge caps. Cast-iron perimeter railings and rainwater goods. Dense bituminous roadway pavement with dressed whinstone and granite kerbstones.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Lush mid-summer (June) urban canopy. Dominant *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore) and *Tilia cordata* (Small-leaved Lime) displaying full, dense green leaf cover, obscuring lateral sightlines. Understory ivy (*Hedera helix*) creeping along stone boundary walling.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All low-poly mesh artifacts, parked vehicles, street bins, traffic cones, and transient road debris fully purged. Ground plane restored to continuous asphalt roadbed, granite kerbs, and stone-flagged pedestrian pavement.
