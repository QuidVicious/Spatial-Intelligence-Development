# Spatial Domain Analysis: 15 Queen St, Edinburgh EH2 1JE, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock**: Carboniferous sedimentary sequence (Gullane Formation, Strathclyde Group) over volcanic basalt sill base.
- **Lithics**: Tooled Craigleith and Hailes quartzose sandstone (buff to pale grey).
- **Mortar & Foundation**: Traditional non-hydraulic lime-sand mortar; glacial till substrate with natural gravity drainage.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Typology**: Classical Georgian New Town continuous terraced townhouses (c. 1780–1820).
- **Massing & Height**: 3 storeys plus rusticated basements with sunken lightwells and dormered attic floors.
- **Planar Rectification**: Facades plumbed to vertical planes; continuous horizontal stringcourses, eaves, and stone band courses.
- **Fenestration**: Regular rhythmic grid of 6-over-6 and 12-pane timber sash-and-case windows.
- **Roofs**: Pitched roofs clad in Scottish slate, stone-built chimney stacks with cylindrical terracotta pots, lead-dressed dormers.
- **Skyline Context**: Distant Old Town volcanic ridge skyline featuring masonry tenements and spires.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Facades**: Fine-tooled sandstone ashlar showing historic coal-smoke patina, soot encrustations, and rain-wash runoffs.
- **Roofing**: Ballachulish blue-grey slates with lead ridge rolls and flashings.
- **Ironwork**: Black-painted cast and wrought iron spearhead railings bordering basement wells.
- **Ground**: Bituminous asphalt carriageway, dressed granite kerbstones, and Caithness stone slab pavements.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Canopy Species**: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), and *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
- **Phenology (Late September)**: Mature canopies showing early autumn chlorosis, mixed muted green, ochre, and gold foliage, minimal branch bareness.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Purged all moving and parked automobiles, photogrammetric distortion mesh clutter, pedestrians, and temporary urban elements.
- Retained permanent civil fixtures: stone kerbing, cast-iron railings, road geometries, and fixed trees.
