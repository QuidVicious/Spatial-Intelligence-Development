# Spatial Domain Analysis: 1 W Register St, Edinburgh EH2 2AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean strata comprise Carboniferous sedimentary sandstone and siltstone bedrock of the Inverclyde Group, overlain by dense glacial till. Primary structural masonry consists of local pale buff-grey Craigleith and Hailes sandstones bonded with traditional non-hydraulic lime-sand mortars. Ground conditions feature engineered urban sub-base over drained historic glacial clay deposits.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban square anchored by the 45-metre fluted Craigleith sandstone Melville Monument, featuring a stepped pedestal and Roman Doric column crowned with a colossal stone statue. Surrounding perimeter consists of 4-to-6-storey Georgian and Victorian commercial palazzo blocks and townhouses: rusticated ashlar ground tiers, regularized 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sash-and-case fenestration grids, dentiled eaves cornices, and Ballachulish slate mansard roofs punctuated by dressed ashlar chimney stacks. Modern mid-to-late 20th-century infill blocks feature rectified concrete frames and modular stone veneer grid facades.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Fine-grained Craigleith and Hailes sandstone ashlar, rusticated sandstone basements, precast concrete and polished granite panels on modern infills.
- Weathering/Patina: Localized coal-smoke sulfate encrustation in sheltered entablatures; rain-washed pale reveals across exposed pediments; bio-patina along low stone plinths.
- Roofs: Grey Scottish slate, lead flashing, copper dormer cladding with light verdigris, and zinc-capped flat roof sections.
- Ground: Granite kerbstones, fine-grained asphalt carriageways with recessed steel tram rails, compacted gravel paths, and perimeter wrought-iron spearhead railings.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Mature urban parkland perimeter populated by *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (common lime), and *Betula pendula* (silver birch). Late September phenology: full canopy density exhibiting initial autumn chlorosis, yellowing outer margins, and sporadic early leaf litter across turf and gravel circuits.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All vehicular traffic, double-decker buses, transit vans, pedestrians, advertising hoardings, temporary scaffolds, and movable street furniture are fully removed. Fixed historic ironwork, tram tracks, stone kerbs, and permanent civic infrastructure remain intact.
