The monumental facades are constructed from fine-grained Craigleith blonde sandstone, exhibiting deep, authentic historical coal soot crusting entrenched within the recessed reveals, fluted pilaster grooves, and underside of heavy projecting cornices, contrasting with rain-cleansed buff stone planes. Below, traditional Caithness flagstone footways and whinstone carriage setts flank deep basement lightwells bounded by spear-topped wrought-iron railings, illuminated under a crisp 5600K northern midday sun that casts clean geometric cast shadows along the descending northern topography.
