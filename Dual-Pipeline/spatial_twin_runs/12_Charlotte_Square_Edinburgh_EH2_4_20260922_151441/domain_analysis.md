# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean strata composed of Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock of the Inverclyde and Strathclyde Groups, overlain by dense glacial till. Structural masonry dominated by local Craigleith quartzitic sandstone (pale buff-grey, fine-grained, high-silica marine sandstone) alongside Hailes sandstone for structural bedding and foundational footings. Monument base incorporates polished Scottish red and grey granite courses bedded in high-calcium traditional non-hydraulic slaked lime mortar.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Neoclassical urban ensemble centered on West Register House (former St George's Church, 1814, Robert Reid). Monumental central frontage features a tetrastyle Ionic portico with pediment, surmounted by an engaged attic stage, a peristyle drum with Corinthian colonnette pilasters, and a ribbed copper dome terminating in an ornate stone lantern. Flanking terraces comprise three-storey plus attic and rusticated basement Georgian townhouses (Robert Adam design, 1791). Elevations display rusticated ground-floor arcades with radial voussoirs, smooth ashlar upper levels ordered by giant-order pilasters, continuous string courses, moulded architraves, balustraded parapets, and timber 6-over-6 sash-and-case windows. In the central green, the Prince Albert Memorial (1876, John Steell) features a monumental bronze equestrian figure atop a stepped, multi-tiered granite pedestal with bronze bas-relief friezes.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith pale buff-to-grey ashlar sandstone, exhibiting historic coal-smoke carbon crusting in sheltered relief, wind-scoured friable crusts, and sub-surface rain-washed pale sandstone reveals.
- Dome & Statuary: Weathered copper sheet cladding with uniform pale-green verdigris carbonate patina; monument features cast statuary bronze with localized dark cupric oxidation and green copper staining over red and grey polished granite ashlar steps.
- Roofing: Scottish Ballachulish blue-grey roofing slate in diminishing courses with zinc flashings, lead hip-rolls, and dressed sandstone chimney stacks with terracotta cylindrical pots.
- Ground Surfaces: Winter rye-fescue lawn turf with dormant grass thatch, edged by dressed whinstone and granite kerbstones.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Urban parkland specimens of *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore). Phenological state for late December (winter dormancy): 100% complete defoliation, totally bare branch architecture revealing intricate ramification networks, dark wet bark crusted with dormant nitrophytic lichens (*Xanthoria parietina*), and dormant winter bud clusters.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient vehicles, parked cars, pedestrians, traffic cones, and temporary signage completely excised. Static municipal infrastructure retained: dressed granite kerbs, wrought-iron garden perimeter railings, and permanent architectural fabric.
