# Spatial Domain Analysis: 12 Charlotte Square, Edinburgh EH2 4ET, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
- **Bedrock & Stratigraphy**: Carboniferous sedimentary succession (calciferous sandstone measures and siltstone strata) overlying volcanic subterranean formations of the Midland Valley.
- **Lithics**: 
  - Primary Structural Masonry: Craigleith Sandstone (buff/pale olive-grey, fine-grained quartz-arenite known for high silica content and durability).
  - Backup & Rubble Core: Hailes Sandstone rubble with non-hydraulic lime bonding.
  - Monument Pedestal: Polished intrusive igneous Peterhead pink granite with fine dressed margins.
- **Mortar & Chemistry**: Traditional hot-mixed lime mortar (slaked calcium oxide with sharp river sand aggregate).
- **Subterranean Hydrology**: Cohesive glacial till over sedimentary bedrock; engineered perimeter French drains and graded subsoil under urban garden turf.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
- **Typology**: Neoclassical Georgian civic and residential complex (Adam/Reid master plan).
- **Central Monumental Structure**: West Register House (former St. George's Church, 1811–1814, Robert Reid). Features a monumental tetrastyle Ionic portico, triangular pediment, stepped attic, cylindrical colonnaded drum, and ribbed hemispherical copper dome terminating in a cupola lantern.
- **Flanking Terraces**: 3-storey plus attic and sunken basement Georgian ashlar townhouses; channelled rustication at ground tier, smooth ashlar upper floors, continuous dentiled eaves cornice, and slate mansard roofs with regular dormers.
- **Fenestration**: Rectilinear fenestration grid of 12-pane and 6-over-6 double-hung timber sash-and-case windows with fine astragals.
- **Foreground Monument**: Prince Albert Memorial (Sir John Steell, 1876); bronze equestrian figure atop an architectural stepped granite plinth with subsidiary corner allegorical bronze groupings.
- **Planar Rectification**: All facade lines, columns, and corners plumbed to true vertical; horizontal string courses and entablatures flattened to true planar alignment.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- **Masonry**: Droved and polished Craigleith sandstone ashlar; channelled rusticated ground floors; dressed ashlar stringcourses and copings.
- **Roofing & Metalwork**: Scottish/Welsh grey-blue roofing slates, lead roll ridges and valley gutters, oxidized verdigris green copper dome sheeting, and dark cast-iron railings.
- **Monument**: Cast statuary bronze with natural dark green-brown oxide and localized copper carbonate run-off; polished pink/red Peterhead granite.
- **Ground Surfaces**: Perennial turfgrass (Lolium perenne/Festuca rubra mix), dressed whinstone kerbstones, and smooth tarmac perimeter roadway.
- **Pathology & Weathering**: Localized coal-smoke particulate staining on protected cornice undersides, rain-washed clean ashlar fascias, and pale sulfate efflorescence at sheltered joints.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
- **Taxa**: *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime), *Ulmus glabra* (Wych Elm).
- **Phenology (Late December)**: Complete winter dormancy; 100% defoliated canopy; bare, dark, ramified branch silhouettes allowing unobstructed structural visibility through the crowns.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
- Completely purged of transient elements: zero pedestrians, zero parked/moving motor vehicles, zero temporary traffic cones, dumpsters, or signage. Retained only permanent civil fabric (cast-iron streetlamps, permanent railings, stone kerbs, and mature trees).
