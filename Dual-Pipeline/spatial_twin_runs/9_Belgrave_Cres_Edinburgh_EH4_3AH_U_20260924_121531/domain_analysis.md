# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 Belgrave Cres, Edinburgh EH4 3AH, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
* **Bedrock & Stratigraphy:** Lower Carboniferous sedimentary formations (Calciferous Sandstone Measures / Edinburgh Border Group) overlaid with compact glacial till.
* **Lithic Sources:** Pale blonde Craigleith sandstone and Hailes sandstone (buff to carbonaceous grey quartz arenite).
* **Mortar Chemistry:** Traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic slaked lime mortar with local river-sand aggregates.
* **Subsurface Drainage:** Sloping topography draining south towards the Water of Leith gorge.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
* **Residential Terrace (Foreground Left):** Victorian Italianate/Classical revival terrace (c. 1870s). Three storeys over basement, polished ashlar, prominent polygonal/canted bay window projections, horizontal string courses, molded eaves cornice, and vertical timber-framed 1-over-1 and 2-over-2 sash-and-case windows.
* **Ecclesiastical Edifice (Center Distance):** Gothic Revival church structure. Stepped buttresses, lancet openings with stone tracery, and a square tower terminated with corner pinnacles and open crenellations. Pitched roofs clad in Scottish slate.
* **Rectification:** All vertical masonry lines rectified to true plumb; street-level photogrammetry mesh noise resolved into planar asphalt roadway and dressed stone boundary copings.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
* **Facades:** Fine-axed and stugged blonde Craigleith sandstone ashlar; church nave in coursed snecked sandstone rubble with dressed ashlar dressings.
* **Roofs & Metals:** Scottish Ballachulish slate, rolled lead ridge flashing, and black-painted cast-iron rainwater goods and spearhead railings.
* **Weathering & Pathology:** Selective coal-smoke particulate encrustation in sheltered lintels and string courses, clean rain-washed vertical facades, and localized biological patina along shaded lower plinths.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
* **Canopy Composition:** Deciduous parkland boundary including *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore), *Fraxinus excelsior* (European Ash), and *Tilia × europaea* (Common Lime).
* **Phenology (Late September):** Full canopy density displaying early autumn senescence, featuring peripheral chlorosis, dry ochre and pale yellow leaf margins, and intact upper crown branching.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All transient road vehicles, pedestrians, debris, and low-fidelity geometric photogrammetry artifacts are removed. Restored static fabric includes continuous asphalt paving, granite kerbs, sandstone retaining walls, ironwork, and mature arboricultural specimens.
