# Spatial Domain Analysis: 8 Ainslie Pl, Edinburgh EH3 6AS, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Subterranean Carboniferous sedimentary bedrock overlain by glacial till. Primary building fabric comprises fine-grained, pale blonde to buff Craigleith and Hailes quartzose sandstone. Masonry bound with traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic lime and sharp quartz sand mortar.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Curved Georgian Neo-Classical residential circus terrace (c. 1822, James Gillespie Graham). Massing forms a four-storey continuous concave elevation over sunken basement lightwells. Ground floor exhibits channelled V-jointed ashlar rustication; upper storeys feature polished ashlar with continuous string courses, moulded architraves, Doric/Ionic pilaster rhythm, full entablature, and parapet blocking course. Regular fenestration grid of multi-pane 6-over-6 and 12-over-12 painted timber sash-and-case windows. Pitched roofs clad in Scottish slate with regular sandstone ashlar chimney stacks.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Craigleith pale blonde sandstone ashlar, channel-rusticated basement and ground floor, smooth-dressed ashlar upper storeys, displaying subtle particulate sooting and rain-washed relief.
- Fenestration: White-painted timber sash-and-case window joinery, single glazing.
- Roof: Dark grey Ballachulish slate tiles, zinc ridges, lead roll flashings, sandstone ridge copes.
- Ground & Boundary: Cast-iron spearhead boundary railings on polished sandstone plinths, caithness flagstone basement lightwells, dressed whinstone kerbs, compacted asphalt roadway, and turf garden perimeter.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Central crescent garden interior populated by mature *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane) and *Acer pseudoplatanus* (Sycamore). September 24 late-summer to early-autumn phenology: dense canopies exhibiting initial leaf chlorosis, yellowing outer margins, and early dispersed litterfall over dense *Lolium perenne* and *Festuca rubra* lawn.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All photogrammetry mesh noise, transient vehicles, pedestrians, modern signage, and temporary street clutter are removed. Retained elements are strictly the static Georgian architectural envelope, perimeter ironwork, stone lightwells, kerbs, and mature garden landscaping.
