# Spatial Domain Analysis: 9 St Colme St, Edinburgh EH3 6AA, UK

**View Scope:** FRUSTUM

## 1. Geology & Indigenous Lithics
Bedrock consists of Carboniferous calciferous sandstone and basaltic volcanic sill intrusions. Masonry comprises pale blonde-to-ochre Craigleith and Hailes carboniferous quartz-arenite sandstone. Mortar is traditional non-hydraulic to weakly hydraulic lime blended with sharp river sand. Ground slopes northwest toward the Water of Leith valley with subsurface glacial till over fractured rock drainage.

## 2. Architecture & Planar Rectification
Georgian Neoclassical circus and crescent plan (Moray Estate, James Gillespie Graham). Continuous curved sandstone facades: rusticated ground floor, polished ashlar on first and second floors, sunken lightwells/basements, and pitched mansard attics with lead-cheeked dormers. Planar facade alignment, true vertical fenestration grid with 6-over-6 and 2-over-2 timber sash-and-case windows. Symmetrical chimney stacks with cylindrical clay flue pots punctuate Scottish slate roofs.

## 3. Materials & Environmental Patina
- Facades: Fine-grained Craigleith ashlar, channelled V-joint rustication at base, smooth ashlar upper levels.
- Weathering: Selective urban coal-smoke encrustations along stringcourses and cornices, rain-washed facade panels, localized biological patina on damp sills.
- Roofs: Ballachulish blue-grey slate with lead hips, ridges, and valleys.
- Paving: Granitic and whinstone sett carriageway, Caithness flagstone footways, dressed sandstone kerbs.
- Ironwork: Wrought and cast-iron spearhead railings and lamp overthrows with black oil-paint finish.

## 4. Landscape Ecology & Botanical Canopy
Central circular private garden containing mature *Platanus × acerifolia* (London plane), *Acer pseudoplatanus* (sycamore), and *Tilia × europaea* (common lime). Date-grounded phenology (late December): 100% defoliated canopy, exposed intricate branching silhouettes, bare wet bark, and dense dormant turf understory with peripheral perennial beds.

## 5. Static Civil Fabric Decluttering
All parked vehicles, moving traffic, pedestrians, transient street signs, and modern refuse bins removed. Historic static fabric preserved: continuous stone setts, kerbs, cast-iron perimeter railings, and intact garden core.
