Under rain, the stone absorbs moisture unevenly according to pore structure, shifting from cool buff-grey to deep wet slate-amber.

Capillary water entry induces subsurface oxidation (laminar rust), causing "rust jacking" that cracks the stone copings around lead-poured iron bases.
