### STAGE 1: CENTROID IDENTIFICATION & REJECTION
The typical synthetic output for an Edinburgh New Town terrace reduces the scene to uniform, light-grey limestone, flat manicured lawn, and standard dry architectural symmetry. This representation completely ignores:
1. The distinctive warm, buff-to-honey-grey tonality of Carboniferous **Craigleith and Hailes sandstone**.
2. The legacy of two centuries of coal smoke ("Auld Reekie") that formed blackened gypsum crusts in sheltered masonry recesses.
3. The complex, multi-layered reality of a wet, organic, mid-autumn private communal garden with saturated turf, waterlogged soil, rotting leaf litter, and cast-iron rust jacking.

### STAGE 2: DOMAIN KEY SELECTION & ACTIVATION

#### 1. Geology & Lithology Lens
* **Substrate & Bedrock:** Glacial till (boulder clay) underlying a rich, compaction-prone, dark organic garden loam.
* **Building Fabric:** Ainslie Place (Moray Estate, 1820s) is constructed from fine-grained, silica-cemented **Craigleith sandstone** with ashlar dressing and droved margins. In wet conditions, its dry buff-blonde color darkens significantly to a damp, saturated ochre-grey, exhibiting anisotropic surface water absorption.

#### 2. Civil & Architectural Engineering Lens
* **Boundary System:** Dwarf ashlar retaining wall of dressed sandstone coping stones, into which vertical cast-iron balustrades are seated directly using poured lead caulking.
* **Facade Typology:** Classical revival terrace designed by James Gillespie Graham: channelled V-joint rustication on the ground floor, smooth polished ashlar on the piano nobile and attic levels, with deep projecting cornices, dentils, and cast-iron window balconies.

#### 3. Historical Metallurgy & Material Aging Lens
* **Railing Integrity:** Wrought and cast iron railings, periodically coated in thick gloss black oil/alkyd paint. Micro-fissuring and age have produced "alligatoring" (scale-like paint failure).
* **Rust Jacking:** Atmospheric moisture has penetrated where the iron stanchions enter the stone coping; the expanding iron oxide ($Fe_2O_3 \cdot nH_2O$) exerts massive internal pressure, cracking the sandstone copings radially around the lead sockets.

#### 4. Urban Botany & Micro-Ecology Lens
* **Canopy Flora:** Mature mature sycamores (*Acer pseudoplatanus*), common limes (*Tilia × europaea*), and weeping ash. On October 15, leaves are mid-senescence: mottled chrome-yellow, olive-brown, spotted with *Rhytisma acerinum* (tar spot fungus).
* **Epiphytes:** Corticolous bryophytes (*Hypnum cupressiforme*, *Dicranoweisia cirrata*) clinging to the bark fissures of the south-east-facing tree trunks; green biofilm (*Desmococcus olivaceus*) coating the damp lower risers of the garden boundary plinths.
* **Ground Layer:** Saturated turf intermixed with moss (*Calliergonella cuspidata*), littered with trampled, water-macerated leaves releasing dark brown tannins into puddles.

#### 5. Chemical Weathering & Moisture Mechanics Lens
* **Soot Crusts vs. Wash Zones:** Facades display uneven soiling: rain-washed projecting details (pilasters, string courses) appear clean and pale, whereas sheltered soffits under cornices retain stable, insoluble black gypsum-carbon crusts.
* **Surface Hydrology:** Saturated surfaces generate a specular continuous water film on smooth flagstones, low-angle puddles on gravel paths, and running water rivulets descending the channelled rustication lines.

### STAGE 3: CROSS-DOMAIN SYNERGY ANALYSIS
* **Tannin-Sandstone Interaction:** Senescent sycamore leaves decaying against porous Craigleith stone coping stones leach humic and tannic acids, creating localized reddish-brown chemical tide-marks that contrast with the yellow-green chlorophyte algae in damp crevices.
* **Lead-Iron-Sandstone Galvanic Joint:** Poured lead collars around rusted iron railing footings show micro-gaps filled with trapped rainwater, directly driving localized frost-thaw micro-spalling of the surrounding sandstone edge.
* **Rain-Soot Chromatic Dichotomy:** Continuous autumn rain saturates the stone, deepening the value contrast between the soot-blackened undersides of classical pediments and the glistening, rain-washed honey-blonde face of the sandstone facade.
